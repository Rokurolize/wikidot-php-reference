# REFCORR38 — paired-token scoping across block lines (F-PAIR)

REFCORR38 corrects the reference implementation's handling of `[[span]]`, `[[div]]`, and inline-format pairs whose opener and closer are separated by list, heading, centered-line, blockquote, definition-list, or table block structure. The evidence is the fresh grouped sandbox probes `muse-probe-hunt-pair-task31`, `muse-probe-hunt-pair-blocks`, `muse-probe-hunt-pair-divcross`, and `muse-probe-hunt-pair-inline`, plus the frozen list, inline, and table witnesses already retained in the conformance tree. Probe pages are left in place; no production page was accessed.

## Corrected mechanisms

1. A paired Span may still be scanned across block lines because that is how the fossil grammar tokenizes the source. The pre-existing list-marker edge exception is extended to the two remaining trailing block-marker edges established by the fresh probes: a definition-list separator line and a centered `= ` line. Horizontal whitespace at those edges is not hoisted out of the pair; keeping the edge inside the pair preserves the ownership needed by the later block rules. In particular, an empty marker line after a cross-line span closes as an empty centered/list block on live; it does not leak a literal centered `=` inside a span-styled paragraph, and a definition-list closing edge remains a closing empty item rather than letting the span escape the list.
2. Inline/block crossing repair is generalized at the output boundary. The reference serializer can legitimately contain crossing inline elements because pair rules are scanned before block rules. When an inline element crosses a block boundary, the established libtidy recovery pass now runs for inline elements generally (`strong`, `em`, underline/strike spans, `tt`, `sup`, `sub`, and `span`), not only for the narrow Span/paragraph shape repaired by REFCORR30. Tidy redistributes or drops the fragments in the same way live does: a pair crossing two list items contributes the paired text to each nonempty item, empty marker-only items disappear, simple and advanced table cells drop cross-cell pair fragments, and well-formed non-crossing output is left in the reference frame.
3. `[[div]]` itself remains a block container. The fresh probes confirm that a div crossing centered or quote lines wraps the resulting centered/quoted blocks, a div written on heading lines remains literal heading text, and a div crossing list, ordinary paragraph, and heading lines is consumed without exposing a stray marker. Those div shapes were already reproduced by the existing block rules and are pinned as non-regression evidence; no div parse-rule change was needed. The cross-kind list-to-center and list-to-heading shapes in the same matrix are Span shapes, and their empty-block outcome is part of correction 1.

## Probe outcomes

The task-31 matrix is canonical-equal after correction: list content yields one list item whose content is spanned; the minimal list form disappears; heading content yields three clean headings with only the middle content spanned; both centered forms yield empty centered paragraphs at the marker-only edges and a centered span only around real content. The block matrix is canonical-equal: blockquote lines keep their breaks inside the span; the definition-list span remains inside the list and does not swallow following labels; simple and advanced table cells drop the empty crossing fragments; ordinary paragraph-line and blank-line-separated spans retain their established behavior. The div matrix is canonical-equal for list, heading, center, blockquote, cross-list-heading, cross-list-center, and div-body shapes. The inline matrix is canonical-equal for strong, em, underline, strike, teletype, superscript, and subscript pairs opened and closed on marker-only list lines.

The frozen witnesses are also canonical-equal: `x38-span-cross-item` becomes one span in each of the two list items, `x60` becomes independent strong ranges in the first two items, `x102` becomes independent sup and tt ranges, and `y01`/`y10` close bold at the table-cell boundary rather than leaking malformed crossing tags. Controls `x37-span-in-item`, `x41-strike-in-item`, and `x43` are byte-identical to the pre-correction checkout.
