# CORRECTION-REFCORR42 — inline pairs crossing inside one inline context

REFCORR42 corrects the reference implementation's handling of two paired inline constructs whose source-level scopes cross inside one paragraph, without crossing a block boundary. The evidence is six byte-verified grouped sandbox probes: `muse-probe-hunt-inline-matrix-a`, `muse-probe-hunt-inline-matrix-b`, `muse-probe-hunt-inline-matrix-c`, `muse-probe-hunt-inline-triple-controls`, `muse-probe-hunt-inline-listbound-a`, and `muse-probe-hunt-inline-listbound-b`. The probes carry 38 labeled shapes covering anchor/strong, strong/emphasis, span/strong, span/anchor, emphasis/underline, teletype/strong, superscript/subscript, span/size, span/color, size/color, and span/strikethrough crossings; a properly nested control for each crossed shape; a three-pair overlap; nested-control and adjacent-pair controls; and one two-list-item boundary shape per pair kind. Probe pages are left in place; no production page was accessed.

Live serves libtidy-renested bytes rather than the raw crossed serialization. The re-nesting is generic over the measured inline pairs. For example, `**A1 //B2** C3//` serves `<strong>A1 <em>B2</em></strong> <em>C3</em>`: the inner emphasis is closed before the outer strong close and then reopened for the remainder. Reopened span-family elements keep their measured attributes, as in the underline shape whose reopened `<span style="text-decoration: underline;">` carries the same style. Anchor/strong closes the strong inside the anchor and does not reopen it after the anchor close in the measured shape. Span/anchor instead extends the anchor to the end of the containing span in the measured byte serialization. The triple overlap closes emphasis and strong inside the anchor and leaves the trailing text outside all three pairs. Same-rendered-tag span-family crossings are byte-invisible in the measured shapes because every pair serializes as `<span>` and `</span>`; the served bytes equal their properly nested controls. In two-list-item shapes, the REFCORR38/41 block repair and this inline repair are the same serving pass: the inline elements are distributed into each list item, with trailing remainder fragments measured exactly in the grouped pins.

The correction is confined to `text-wiki/Text/Wiki.php`. The existing malformed-output scan, previously limited to inline elements crossing block-element boundaries, now also detects an inline end tag whose matching opener has another paired-rule inline element still open above it. That detection fires the pre-existing libtidy-backed purifier. No parse-rule pairing, serializer output, corpus file, ftml tree, or production-facing artifact was changed. Properly nested pairs, same-tag controls, and adjacent non-overlapping pairs do not themselves trigger the detector; in grouped live probes they remain byte-identical under the same serving pass.

`cases.jsonl`, `expected.json`, `captures.json`, and `verify_refcorr42.py` pin six grouped live-probe cases. All 6 expected-render byte pins and all 6 live-canonical pins pass in this checkout. Since the measured live bytes and the reference bytes are exact for the grouped pins, the canonical pins are a second comparison class rather than a substitute for byte equality.
