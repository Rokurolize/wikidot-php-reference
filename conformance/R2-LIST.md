# R2 List and Deflist stage: differential and correction catalog (2026-11-20)

Method. This stage runs as a team of two rule owners plus a root coordinator, one owner for each of the thirty-third and thirty-fourth pipeline rules in this batch: List and Deflist, the list family. The loop is building a PHP reference implementation that reproduces live Wikidot behavior, the fossil Text_Wiki snapshot corrected rule by rule against live evidence: the fossil is a skeleton and never evidence, live Wikidot behavior is definitionally correct, and the evidence order is ftml corpus pins first, then frozen captures, then live sandbox probes, with fetching from live pre-approved and cached once. Earlier stages are published as the catalogs conformance-refphp/R2-INCLUDE.md, R2-FRAME.md, R2-CODE.md, R2-FORM-RAW.md, R2-MODULE.md, R2-NEXT.md, R2-LINK.md, R2-NOTES.md, R2-BLOCK.md and R2-CONTAIN.md, and this catalog imitates their shape. Each owner reads its rule's parse and render files and the corresponding ftml behavior, builds an at-least-40-case whole-document battery covering its matching space, runs the reference before and after correction through the shared harness (ref-php/harness/batch.php, modes render and tokens) and the stage's shared ftml dumper (conformance-refphp/list/rust-dump/, preprocess_for_layout, tokenize, parse, HtmlRender over JSONL {"id","source", optional "tags"} at the read-only ftml checkout HEAD recorded beside it), probes live under its disjoint prefix (muse-probe-r2list-list- and muse-probe-r2list-def-), corrects only its own two rule files while preserving the pre-correction bytes as <rule>/<File>.before.php, classifies every correction and every remaining disagreement, and writes its SECTION.md ending in COMPLETE or BLOCKED:. The root owns the shared harness and dumper, the cross-rule interactions, the catalog, and the final smoke run; the root does not do an owner's rule work except where an owner leaves it unfinished and the catalog says so. Prose is written in flowing paragraphs on single lines, never hard-wrapped. The final line of this catalog is the exact watcher work-in-progress marker until integration is truly complete.
