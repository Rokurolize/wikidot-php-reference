# Rule: Embed (`[[embed]]`, R3.17)

- **Battery**: 82 cases (`evidence/embed/battery.jsonl`: 62 authored `eb-*` cases + 20 live probe cases `p01`..`p20`), plus 6 supplementary live probes `se01`..`se06` (`evidence/embed/supplementary.jsonl`, captures in `evidence/embed/probes2-captures.json`).
- **Live probes**: 20 pages `muse-probe-refval-embed-p01`..`p20` plus 6 pages `muse-probe-refval2-embed-se01`..`se06` on `sandbox-for-codex`, all with `saved_source_matches=true`, `valid=true`, `stable=true`.
- **Corrections (2)**, both in `ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Embed.php` (pre-correction backup `Embed.before-refval2.php`), inside the branch where a pattern from `wikidot-root/conf/wikiparser/embed/` has matched:
  1. **Dangerous scheme in `src` renders empty**: when the matched content's `src` attribute value, trimmed of leading whitespace and lowercased, begins with `javascript:` or `vbscript:`, the token renders as the empty string instead of passing the content through. The fossil passed a `javascript:` iframe through verbatim because the any-iframe pattern matches any attributed iframe. Settled by `p17` (`javascript:alert(1)`), `se02` (`JaVaScRiPt:`, case-insensitive), `se04` (`vbscript:`), and `se06` (leading space before the scheme).
  2. **Dangerous scheme in any other attribute value is a no-match**: when any other attribute value contains `javascript:` or `vbscript:` (case-insensitive), the render falls through to the standard `<div class="error-block">Sorry, no match for the embedded content.</div>`. Settled by `se03` (a benign `http:` src with `data-x="javascript:foo"`).
- **Non-corrections pinned by the same probes**: a `data:text/html;base64,...` src passes through unchanged (`se05`), so the scheme check is specifically the script schemes, not a scheme allowlist; the fossil's event-attribute rejection (`p14`, an `onclick` attribute gives the error block on both sides) and its whitespace-only iframe body requirement (`p16`, fallback text gives the error block on both sides) already matched live, as did the parse rule's acceptance of mixed open/close tag names (`p04`..`p06`) and the benign passthrough shapes (`p12`, `p13`, `p15`, `se01`).
- **Comparison before vs. after**: before correction, 15 of 20 probes differed (14 of them only in the Paragraph-wrap framing of the error block, plus the substantive `p17`) and 5 were trim-equal. After correction, the reference agrees with live on 20/20 probes and 6/6 supplementary probes under frame normalization (the error-block cases under the Stage 2 div-in-paragraph split, and the empty-render cases against live's dropped empty paragraph; `evidence/verify-refval2.py`). Battery movement from the pre-correction renders is exactly 2 cases, `eb-x13` and `p17`, the two `javascript:`-src cases in the battery.
- **Smoke check**: 9/9 smoke cases byte-identical before and after the REFVAL2 corrections (swap-verified against the `*.before-refval2.php` copies).
- **R3 stage consequence**: none. `Parse/Default/Embed.php` is unchanged; both corrections are in the Xhtml render.
