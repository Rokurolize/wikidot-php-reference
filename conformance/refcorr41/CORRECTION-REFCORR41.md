# CORRECTION-REFCORR41 — anchor pairs crossing block boundaries

REFCORR41 corrects the reference implementation's handling of paired `[[a ...]]...[[/a]]` anchors whose opener and closer are separated by block structure. The evidence is six byte-verified grouped sandbox probes: `muse-probe-hunt-anchor-blockinc`, `muse-probe-hunt-anchor-listtable`, `muse-probe-hunt-anchor-headcenter`, `muse-probe-hunt-anchor-bqdefpara`, `muse-probe-hunt-anchor-controls`, and `muse-probe-hunt-anchor-tabledisc`. The probes carry 37 labeled shapes covering missing-include blocks, div blocks, list wrappers and item crossings, simple and advanced tables, headings, centered lines, blockquotes, definition lists, paragraph soft lines and blank lines, the reduced KO `semiotics-hub` include shape, single-bracket and triple-bracket controls, same-paragraph and soft-line controls, and the REFCORR38 protected controls. Probe pages are left in place; no production page was accessed.

Live serves the same libtidy-redistributed anchor shape observed in the cached KO `semiotics-hub` capture: an anchor opened before a paragraph-ending block is closed in the preceding empty or content paragraph and reopened inside the block. In lists the anchor is distributed into each item, including marker-only items. In definition lists it is distributed into the term and definition entries. In centered lines each line receives its own anchor. In headings and tables malformed crossing fragments are removed or closed at the structural boundary. A same-paragraph anchor pair and an anchor pair split only by soft line breaks are unchanged. Single-bracket links and triple-bracket page links are not pair syntax and remain controls only.

The correction has three parts. `text-wiki/Text/Wiki.php` adds the anchor tag to the REFCORR38 malformed-output detector, so the existing tidy-backed purifier runs when an anchor crosses a block boundary. `text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Anchor.php` preserves the horizontal whitespace after a trailing list, definition-list, center, heading, or blockquote marker inside the pair body, because those block rules recognize only `* `, `: term : `, `= `, `+ `, or `> ` marker lines; without that space the closing anchor token falls outside the block and live's redistribution cannot be reproduced. The same rule keeps the blank-line paragraph redistribution away from bodies containing a line table: the table is a block boundary, not a plain paragraph run, and splitting there prevented table recognition. `text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Paragraph.php` treats Anchor and Span delimiter tokens as layout while classifying an otherwise empty lead into a skipped block; visible text still disqualifies the empty lead. This is the simple-table lead mechanism measured by both the anchor shapes and the span control: live serves `<a...><br /></a>` or `<span><br /></span>` bare before the table rather than wrapping that break-only lead in `<p>`.

`cases.jsonl`, `expected.json`, `captures.json`, and `verify_refcorr41.py` pin six grouped live-probe cases. All 6 expected-render byte pins and all 6 live-canonical pins pass in this checkout. The canonical comparison uses the established census mask on both sides, including its normalization of generated heading anchor numbers. The individual reproduction artifacts in the lane directory record first-diff positions for the reduced shapes; controls that were already correct are byte-identical.

The parser corrections are limited to `text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Anchor.php` and `text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Paragraph.php`; the output-recovery trigger correction is limited to `text-wiki/Text/Wiki.php`. The conformance record is contained in this directory. No corpus file, ftml tree, render rule for a paired token, or production-facing artifact was changed.
