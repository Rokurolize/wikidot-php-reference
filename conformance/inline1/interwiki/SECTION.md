Mechanism as corrected. The parse rule keeps its site:page regex and its two bracketed forms, lowercases the site for the described form, and checks the configured site table before claiming a standalone link; the render rule looks the site up in the render table, expands the %s slot, HTML-escapes the label and emits the onclick target fragment when configured. Neither rule file was corrected: both remain byte-identical to the canonical record and to the preserved before-copies (parse SHA-256 5f47c8e77541170b172df86783b4c2cffcac6df0e295794dedb689a657f8a75e, render e9137aa7a3bc8969cff9d6dc0a7ab5f8c5d00619a3b9893a1319232238d56a1a).
