# R2-inline1 Url rule: differential and correction section (2026-11-20)

Method. I compared three sides over a 75-case whole-document battery (battery.jsonl, make_battery.py) covering schemes, hosts, ports, query and fragment forms, trailing punctuation and parenthesis balance, described links, star links, double-bracket targets with and without pipes, raw adjacency, and malformed or unterminated forms. The reference side is the shared harness ref-php/harness/batch.php in render and tokens modes, captured before and after as ref-render-before.jsonl, ref-render-after.jsonl, ref-tokens-before.jsonl and ref-tokens-after.jsonl with empty stderr sidecars; the ftml side is the stage's one shared dumper at conformance-refphp/inline1/rust-dump/target/release/r2inline1-rust-dump over the same battery at the recorded HEAD f9dc0e410, in ftml-dump.jsonl. Live evidence is the coordinator's anonymous saved-page view of muse-probe-r2in1-x-x04 (a double-bracket URL target carrying a pipe) and muse-probe-r2in1-x-xstar (a starred autolink), fetched directly and cached under conformance-refphp/inline1/quicklive/; the owner's own probe pages were created under muse-probe-r2in1-url- but were not captured before handoff, which is a stated coverage gap rather than padded evidence. The root completed this section from the on-disk evidence after the owner stopped, and says so here.

Mechanism as corrected. The parse rule still recognises the fossil's scheme list plus six schemes that live Wikidot autolinks and ftml carries in its Wikidot-only scheme table: ssh, git, sftp, svn, telnet and cvs (C1). Bare URLs are matched by the fossil's regex, a leading asterisk sets target="_blank", described links keep their labelled form, and a double-bracket URL target keeps any pipe inside the target. The render rule emits target="_blank" for a starred link rather than the fossil's onclick window.open fragment, while keeping the suppression of targets on on-page anchors and mailto links (C3). A bare URL now stops at an inline-math opener, and a pipe immediately before that opener belongs to the label surface rather than the target (C4); a described URL whose label contains an inline-math opener yields the whole match to the later Mathinline rule (C5). Both are live-settled by root probes x45 and x02 respectively, which the corrected reference now reproduces byte-for-byte. An earlier owner edit (C2) split a double-bracket target at a pipe to follow ftml; the coordinator's live probe muse-probe-r2in1-x-x04 shows live rendering `<a href="http://example.com|link">http://example.com|link</a>` byte-for-byte as the pre-correction reference did, confirming the standing F-R2-44 finding in the opposite direction from the edit, so C2 was reverted and is reported here as a withdrawn attempt rather than a correction.

Corrections and classifications. C1 is a fossil-era artifact: the six added schemes are present in ftml's url.rs WIKIDOT_ONLY_AUTOLINK_SCHEMES and its link_single scheme walk, and live's autolinking of the schemes ftml labels Wikidot-only is the parity side's settled reading; no dedicated live probe page was captured for each of the six, so the classification rests on ftml source rather than a per-scheme live capture, and that is a coverage gap. C3 is a fossil-era artifact settled live by muse-probe-r2in1-x-xstar, which renders `<a href="https://example.com/star-target" target="_blank">...` exactly; the owner's citation of a corpus pin named test/link/star-killed-autolink-fallback/wikidot.html was not verified against the checkout and should be read as a pointer to ftml's url.rs tests rather than as a literal fixture path. C4 and C5 are reference bugs on live-settled ownership seams, evidenced by root probes x45 and x02 and by ftml's wikidot_automatic_url_end and explicit-link-label pin; they are narrow boundary fixes and leave plain pipe-bearing targets untouched. Both changed files have their exact pre-correction bytes preserved as Url.php.before.php and UrlXhtml.before.php. The withdrawn C2 is not a correction.

Battery and probe numbers. Of 75 battery cases the corrected reference and the dumper agree on 65 raw outputs and 66 after frame normalization, up from 51 and 52 before correction; all six driver outputs have empty stderr sidecars. The changed records are the six added-scheme cases, the star cases and the math-boundary cases. The live direct GETs and the root interaction captures settle their shapes: x04 agrees with the corrected reference against ftml, xstar agrees with all three sides, and x02 and x45 now agree on all three sides after C4 and C5. No owner-prefix live capture set exists for this rule, so scheme-by-scheme and described-link live numbers are not claimed.

Remaining disagreements and dispositions. The pipe-bearing double-bracket target (battery u57-u60 and x04) is the already-routed F-R2-44 ftml divergence: live and the reference include the pipe in the target, ftml stops at it. The non-breaking-space case u31 differs because ftml percent-encodes the byte in the href while live and the reference keep it literal; this is routed below as a candidate finding. The em-dash tail u40 and the described double-space u49 are frame-level typography and whitespace surface owned by later rules, not Url divergences. The angle-raw adjacency u68 belongs to the F-R2-7 raw-family interaction already closed on the parity side. The described-link-with-math ownership seam x02 and the pipe-plus-math seam x45 were live-settled by root's interaction battery and corrected here in C4 and C5, so they are no longer residuals. One question arose and is recorded rather than silently resolved: whether the six added schemes deserve per-scheme live captures; the assumption proceeded on is that ftml's explicit Wikidot-only scheme list is sufficient evidence for the match, and the gap is stated above.
