# R2-inline1 Mathinline rule: differential and correction section (2026-11-20)

Method. I compared three sides over a 56-case whole-document battery (battery.jsonl, make_battery.py) covering delimiter spacing, empty and padded bodies, dollar and special characters, HTML and entity content, Unicode, label-like and attribute-like content, newline and blank-line bodies, nested and unterminated forms, and neighbors in spans, headings, table cells, code and math blocks. The reference side is ref-php/harness/batch.php in render and tokens modes with before and after captures (ref-render-before.jsonl, ref-render-after.jsonl, ref-tokens-before.jsonl, ref-tokens-after.jsonl, plus UTF-8 token variants and the evidence-only tokens_utf8.php), all with empty stderr sidecars; the ftml side is the stage's shared dumper at conformance-refphp/inline1/rust-dump/target/release/r2inline1-rust-dump at HEAD f9dc0e410. Live evidence is 27 saved anonymous page views under muse-probe-r2in1-mi-, created and captured with the bundled wikidot-probe CLI and cached in probes/captures.json with the input and log files; all 27 are valid, stable and source-matching. Evidence order was ftml corpus pins, then frozen captures, then live probes, with live winning every disagreement it settled. The root wrote this section from the on-disk evidence after the owner stopped, and says so here.

Mechanism as corrected. The parse rule still matches `[[$ ... $]]` and trims the body, but it now resolves the `\xFF<id>\xFF` placeholders that earlier Newline and Raw rules can leave inside the LaTeX source: each placeholder is looked up in the wiki token array, and a placeholder whose token is a newline or a raw span suppresses the content to the empty string rather than being carried into the math token. This reproduces live's `$$` span for single-newline, newline-after-opener, newline-before-closer and raw-inside shapes. The render rule no longer builds a md5 PNG path through the stubbed LatexRenderer and no longer emits the fossil error-inline span; it emits the deterministic legacy span `<span class="math-inline">$escaped-content$</span>`, escaping HTML metacharacters with ftml's exact bytes (`&amp;`, `&lt;`, `&gt;`, `&quot;`, `&#39;`, NUL to a space). Live's basic probe renders `A [[$ x^2 $]] B` as a span byte-identically to ftml, and even a working LaTeX renderer would have made the fossil emit an img, so this is a fossil-era mechanism change and not a stub repair. The Math block render remains byte-identical from the R2-NEXT stage because live's block form is a generated equation image, a genuinely serving-layer computation.

Battery and probe numbers. On the 56-case battery the parse-shape agreement with ftml rose from 52 of 56 to 56 of 56, with exactly four token contents changed (single-newline, newline-after-opener, newline-before-closer, raw-inside), and raw full-render agreement rose from 7 of 56 to 46 of 56; under the root comparator's canonicalization agreement rose from 7 of 56 to 47 of 56, while the owner's looser frame normalization reaches 52 of 56, the remainder being wrapper and newline surface. All driver stderr sidecars are empty. Of the 27 valid live probes, 20 are source-matched to battery cases and 18 of those 20 agree three-way canonically; the residuals are the in-div wrapper, the math-block-then-inline adjacency where live and ftml render the block equation while the harness takes the stubbed block failure path, and frame whitespace. Live probes for the four placeholder cases render `$$` exactly as the corrected reference and ftml do.

Remaining disagreements and dispositions. The residuals are all frame-level surface owned by later rules: the `<div >` and `<br />` spacings, the paragraph and heading wrapper seams, and the block-Math serving-layer error path that the R2-NEXT stage settled and that this stage does not reopen. No candidate ftml divergence was found for this rule. A scope gap is stated: five extra escaping probes (quotes, greater-than, multiple spaces, tab padding and entity content) were listed but not created before the stop order, so their escaping is pinned by ftml dumper output and by the live html-chars probe rather than by dedicated captures. One question was recorded rather than silently resolved: whether the render change was serving scope or a live-settled fossil artifact; the coordinator adjudicated that even a working renderer would emit the obsolete img form, and that decision is recorded above.
