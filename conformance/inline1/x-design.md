# Root cross-rule interaction design, R2 inline1 stage

The four per-rule batteries own their own matching spaces; this battery owns the seams between them. The design hypotheses are: (1) a bare URL or mailto form inside a described-link label, or a URL inside a double-bracket target, is claimed by whichever inline rule the tokenizer reaches first, and the F-R2-6/F-R2-44 pair shows the pipe splits on context; (2) an inline math form is claimed by Mathinline wherever an enclosing rule leaves its body open (span, heading, table cell), but stays literal inside Code and Raw spans because earlier rules shield them; (3) an interwiki prefix collides with Freelink and include's site-pair grammar only when the surrounding bracket form belongs to those rules, and the configured site table decides whether the form is a link or literal text; (4) autolinks inside Code and Raw spans are shielded by pipeline order on the reference side and by literal-range exclusion in ftml. Root captures stage-start and final-tree reference render and token output over xbattery.jsonl, shared-dumper output at the pinned ftml HEAD, and cached anonymous saved-page captures under muse-probe-r2in1-x-. Probe sources are LF-only with no trailing newline because Wikidot strips one on save. The comparator uses the campaign-standard probe.norm masking plus local id masks and reports a family-shape signature (link href and label shape, email anchor presence, math-inline span shape, interwiki href shape, p and br structure, literal unmatched constructs and visible text) alongside whole-string canonical agreement under known frame-surface normalization. A case is called all-three only when the three signatures agree; reference-plus-live against ftml is the pattern that becomes a candidate finding for the coordinator. Assumptions proceeded on: the ftml checkout HEAD is the recorded f9dc0e410 for every dumper record this stage; LaTeX expansion and attachment existence remain serving-layer scope and are normalized away; frame-level p and br surface already routed in earlier stages is not re-adjudicated here; untagged pages only, because the probe tool creates untagged pages. Unsettled questions found during the run are recorded in the catalog rather than silently assumed.
