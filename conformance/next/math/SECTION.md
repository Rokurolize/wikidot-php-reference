## Mechanism as corrected

Parse rule (ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Math.php). The rule still matches a line-start `[[math label attrs]]` opener with a case-insensitive [a-z0-9_]* label, zero or more `key="value"` attributes whose key charset remains [a-z0-9]+, a newline after the opener, and a newline before a case-insensitive `[[/math]]` closer followed by whitespace or end of input; it still trims the body, takes an equation id from the wiki's math_id variable, emits a Math token surrounded by two newlines, and maps the label into the static equations array. Two changes were made. The body group's recursion now carries the negative lookahead `(?!\s*\[\[/math\]\])`, so a nested `[[math ...]]` block is consumed recursively only when its closer is not immediately followed by another `[[/math]]` closer; an adjacent closer pair leaves the first closer to the outer block and the second as literal text, which is the live shape. The `[[math a-b]]` and `[[math a.b]]` labels and the `foo-bar="x"` and `foo_bar="x"` attribute forms remain literal: live confirms the attribute-key rejection and ftml is the side that diverges there, so no charset broadening was made. The \include/\input guard now compiles and fires under modern PCRE, so a body containing `include{` or `input{` (with or without a visible backslash) throws a catchable ProcessException and the harness records the error instead of silently rendering the equation.

Render rule (ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Math.php). This file is byte-identical to its pre-correction copy Math.render.before.php (SHA-256 88a3fcdf334e2ab70b0de2ea81484780b33c8c001e8ce4c0edc02f3a57f4a008) and was not edited. It still hashes the trimmed body and type, asks Ozone for the site's local math directory, invokes the root-owned no-op LatexRenderer stub, and emits either the fossil "The equation has not been processed correctly. Most prabably it has syntax error(s)." error block or the equation-number span plus img markup with an id of the form equation-<id_prefix><id>. Because the stub writes no PNG, every reference Math render takes the error-block path; real LaTeX image generation and the environment chosen from the body or type attribute are serving-layer concerns outside this stage's scope.

## Corrections made

C1 (PHP version artifact, preserved as Math.parse.before.php with SHA-256 37a992303cf4d48785a93b328bfd380eeb5fa08ffebfb580bca71ac97d03abeb before editing). The fossil guard was `preg_match(';\include\s*{|\input\s*{;is', $content)`, but PCRE2 rejects the unknown `\i` escape at compilation, so preg_match returned false with a suppressed warning on PHP 7.3+/8 and the ProcessException branch was dead: battery case include-brace rendered an equation rather than an error. I replaced it with `;include\s*\{|input\s*\{;is`, which restores the fossil-era PCRE1 semantics in which an unrecognized `\i` was a literal `i`. This changed five battery render outputs (include-brace, input-brace, include-in-word, include-uppercase, include-in-label-block) from the failure-path error block to `ProcessException: Invalid LaTeX expression(s) found.` records; include-space and input-space stay unchanged because they have no brace. One question arose and is recorded here rather than silently resolved: live's save filter refuses \include sources, so no live render capture can settle whether a bare `include{...}` without a backslash is rejected at render time; I preserved the apparent fossil-era semantics rather than inventing a narrower shape, and did not edit ftml on this point.

C2 (fossil-era artifact manifesting as a reference bug, same preserved before bytes). The fossil body group `((?:(?R)|.)*?)` recursed into a nested `[[math]]` block and swallowed its closer, so battery case nested-two produced a token body of `[[math]]\nx\n[[/math]]` with no literal closer left. The ftml corpus pins test/math/nested-closer-run, nested-closer-adjacent-pair, nested-end-first-closer, nested-end-swallowed-closers, nested-end-triple-nest and nested-closer-merge show that live lets an immediately following closer terminate the outer block while a closer with intervening text is consumed by the nested block, and live probes muse-probe-r2next-math-nested-two and -nested-outer-tail confirm the exact bodies. Adding `(?!\s*\[\[/math\]\])` to the recursive alternative reproduces that rule without changing non-nested blocks: nested-two and nested-labels now leave the second closer literal, nested-outer-tail is unchanged, and the scratch check of all six corpus-pin nesting shapes matched their live bodies. This changed two battery render outputs and one probe render output. No PHP-version artifact or harness matter required a Math-rule correction; the original missing LatexRenderer::setDensity() that made the mathinline-after case fatal was harness matter and was fixed by root in the shared stub, not by a Math file.

## Battery and probe numbers

The battery is 76 cases covering line-start and inline openers, label charset/case/empties, type and other attributes, body newlines and trailing whitespace, nesting and recursion, malformed and unterminated forms, \include/\input bodies, and adjacency with Iftags, Comment, Date, Equationreference, Mathinline, Div, Blockquote and Collapsible in pipeline order. Before correction the reference and ftml agreed on the recognized-versus-literal shape in 74 of 76 cases, the two exceptions being attr-hyphen-key and attr-underscore-key; after correction they agree in 69 of 76, with seven disagreements made up of those two attribute-key cases and the five now-caught \include/\input cases. Seven of 76 render records changed (two from C2, five from C1), five after records are caught ProcessException errors, and every stderr sidecar is empty. In tokens mode the two nesting bodies changed as intended and the five guarded cases no longer emit a Math token; all other token shapes are unchanged. Of the 69 after-agreements, 50 are math-block agreements whose rendered HTML differs only by the out-of-scope LaTeX image surface and frame shaping, which is the expected serving-layer difference.

Of the 28 cases in the final live probe set, 25 produced valid stable captures with byte-identical saved sources; the three \include/\input sources were not captured because one save was refused by the save filter and the other two were dropped preemptively for the same reason. Under shape canonicalization of the reference error block versus live and ftml equation markup, 23 of 25 agree on all three sides, 2 agree on reference and live against ftml (the attribute-key cases F1), and none agree on ftml and live alone or have all three disagree. The battery's two attribute-key disagreements are therefore settled by live in favor of the reference, and the five \include/\input disagreements are a coverage gap rather than a live-adjudicated reference error.

## Remaining disagreements, dispositions, and candidate findings

Class S (frame-level surface owned by later rules, not Math divergences): the reference's "equation has not been processed correctly" error block versus ftml's and live's equation markup, which is the stubbed serving-layer failure path; the environment chosen from the body or a type attribute, visible as ftml and live "Unsupported math environment" blocks for type-uppercase and type-empty while the reference emits its generic failure block; the trailing newlines the reference leaves around literal and block content; ftml's `<br>` versus the reference's `<br />`; the wrapper DOM around Math inside Div, Collapsible and Blockquote; and the Mathinline adjacency whose reference render error was a harness matter already fixed by root. These were not corrected and are not candidate ftml findings.

F1 (candidate ftml divergence, route to coordinator). ftml's parse_wikidot_math_head at src/parsing/rule/impls/block/blocks/math.rs accepts attribute keys made of ASCII alphanumerics, underscore or hyphen, so `[[math foo-bar="x"]]` and `[[math foo_bar="x"]]` render as equations in ftml, while live probes muse-probe-r2next-math-attr-hyphen-key and -attr-underscore-key render literal paragraphs and the fossil regex agrees with live. Battery cases attr-hyphen-key and attr-underscore-key show the same split. No reference change was made and no ftml file was edited.

F2 (candidate ftml divergence with a coverage gap, route to coordinator). ftml renders \include and \input bodies as ordinary LaTeX text inside an equation (battery include-brace, input-brace, include-in-word, include-uppercase, include-in-label-block), while the restored fossil guard rejects them and live Wikidot's save filter refused to store the corresponding probe pages. Because live rendering is not observable for these sources, I record this as a candidate for root's triage and a stated coverage gap, and I do not call ftml wrong on a shape live has settled.

Cross-rule cases left for root are in x-notes.txt: Iftags suppression on an untagged page, Comment spans hiding or crossing a Math opener, Date adjacency, Equationreference numbering, Mathinline adjacency, and later wrapper rules. Suppressed content from later rules and Date or Math inside suppressed regions are root's cross-rule integration and were noted rather than owned here. No ftml file, shared harness file, git commit or git identity variable was changed by this rule owner.
