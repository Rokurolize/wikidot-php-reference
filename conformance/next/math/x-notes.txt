R2-next Math cross-rule notes for root (cases I leave to your integration)

These are Math-adjacent cases in my battery whose outcome depends on a rule I do not own. I ran them so the interactions are visible, but I did not correct any file outside ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Math.php.

Suppressed content and earlier pipeline rules. Battery case in-iftags ([[iftags poll]] wrapping a math block) produces an empty body in both the reference and ftml on an untagged page; the tag-gated polarity needs a tagged-page context under your muse-probe-r2next-x- prefix and is yours to probe. Battery case after-comment ([!-- hidden --] before a math block) and comment-in-body (a comment span carrying a stray [[/math]] inside an equation body) both produce a recognized math block on both sides, and live probe muse-probe-r2next-math-comment-in-body confirms the block; a comment that hides a Math opener is still a cross-rule case for you. Battery case after-date uses a bare [[date 1216153821]] before a math block; the date context is serving-layer and the Math block shape is unaffected, confirmed by live probe muse-probe-r2next-math-after-date.

Later pipeline rules and frame-level surface. Battery cases eref-after and eref-before pair [[math E]] with [[eref E]]; Equationreference runs after Math and is not my rule. I observed the token-level label/id behavior but left Equationreference's parse and render untouched; live probe muse-probe-r2next-math-eref-after renders the equation block. Battery case mathinline-after combines a Math block with [[$ y $]]; Mathinline renders after Math. Root added a no-op setDensity() to the shared LatexRenderer stub after I reported that the missing method made this case fatal; it now renders the failure-path inline span, and I classify the original failure as harness matter fixed by root, not a Math correction. Battery cases in-div, in-collapsible and in-blockquote show Math inside later wrapper rules; the equation markup itself is stable and the wrapper DOM is serving-layer/frame surface owned by those rules. Live probes muse-probe-r2next-math-in-div and -in-collapsible confirm the block survives both wrappers.

Nesting inside a Math body. Battery cases nested-two, nested-outer-tail and nested-labels contain a second [[math]] opener inside the body. I corrected the Math rule's recursion lookahead myself because the behavior is Math-owned; live probes muse-probe-r2next-math-nested-two and -nested-outer-tail agree with the ftml corpus pins. If a suppressed Iftags or Comment region wraps one of these closer-run shapes, that combination remains your integration surface.

Candidate ftml divergences for your catalog. Live probes muse-probe-r2next-math-attr-hyphen-key and -attr-underscore-key show a literal paragraph for [[math foo-bar="x"]] and [[math foo_bar="x"]], agreeing with the reference while ftml renders an equation; this is my F1. Live Wikidot's save filter refused pages containing \include{...} (AMC savePage not_ok), so the reference's restored ProcessException branch on \include/\input bodies has no live render capture; this is my F2 and a coverage gap, not a claim that ftml is wrong on a shape live has settled.

Serving-layer scope. Every Math render in the reference takes the harness LatexRenderer failure path and emits the fossil error block; ftml and live emit the real equation markup or an unsupported-environment error. The LaTeX image itself and environment selection from the body are out of scope, so none of those differences are Math-rule corrections.
