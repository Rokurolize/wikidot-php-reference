# CORRECTION C-SUSPECT-RAWHEAD-1/2/3/4 — Raw scanner run shapes and cross-line forms (REFCORR3 lane, 2026-11-22)

Status: APPLIED in this tree by adoption of the SUSPECT-RAWHEAD lane's corrected scanner. The reference's R2-corrected Raw parse rule collapsed a bare three-@ run, refused a three-@ opener or a three-or-more-@ closer on the cross-line form, and rtrimmed a multi-newline body into a false match; live keeps the bare three, accepts three-run openers and surplus-@ closers, and aborts on a multi-newline body. The four divergences were confirmed with fresh live captures in the SUSPECT-RAWHEAD lane (REPORT-SUSPECT-RAWHEAD.md, conformance-suspect-rawhead/CANDIDATES.md) and are recorded in GAP-LEDGER.md.

## The settling evidence (fresh live probes, all valid, stable, and saved-source-matching)

Twenty-one probes on sandbox-for-codex (conformance-suspect-rawhead/cases-all.jsonl, probe-map.json, captures.json) fix the four rules. bare-at3 keeps all three @ where the old scanner emitted nothing; bare-at5/6/7 pin the unchanged run-minus-four count. xline-open3 and xline-open3b show a three-run opener forming a cross-line Raw with body "@x", where the old early exit consumed the opener; xline-open4/4b pin the run>=4 early exit. xline-close3, xline-close4, xline-close3-nospace and xline-close3b show that a closer run of three or more closes and leaves the surplus @ literal after one space, even when no source space follows the closer. xline-two-nl and xline-empty2 show that a body with two or more newlines aborts the construct, leaving the opener and closer literal. form-tabindent had no valid capture and is excluded from both counts. Seventeen of the twenty-one captures now match byte-for-byte after the correction (eight before); the four residuals are three documented frame-surface cases (head-seven, head-space-star, toc-level1) and the uncorrected C-SUSPECT-RAWHEAD-5.

## The correction (reference)

Parse/Default/Raw.php only, adopted verbatim from the lane's corrected file. The early exit for a long @ run is narrowed to run >= 4 and its kept count is $run for a bare three-run and $run-4 otherwise; the cross-line branch accepts a closer run >= 2, computes the opener's extra @ locally, prepends one space to any surplus closer @, and requires the body to contain exactly one newline (its trailing one) instead of rtrimming.
