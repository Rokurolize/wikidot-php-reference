# R2-notes Part 3: Footnoteblock (fnb-owner, 2026-11-20)

## Mechanism as corrected
Footnoteblock is a token-emitting collector with a document-level fallback. The parse rule has regex null and overrides parse() with a single preg_replace_callback over the whole remaining source; its pattern matches the first [[footnoteblock]] with optional attributes, or, failing that, the empty end-of-string anchor. The callback reads vars['footnotes'] (populated earlier in pipeline order by the Footnote rule, which numbers notes in source order), returns nothing when the list is empty, and otherwise emits a Footnoteblock start token with a title option, then for each collected note a Footnoteitem start token, the note body, two newlines and a Footnoteitem end token, then a Footnoteblock end token. Because it uses limit 1, a second and later footnoteblock is never consumed and survives as literal text, which is exactly what live and ftml do. The end-of-string fallback is what appends a footer when a document has notes but no explicit block, also matching live. The Xhtml render emits, for the start token, <div class="footnotes-footer"> followed by a <div class="title"> unless the title is the empty string, and for the end token </div>; the title defaults to Footnotes via _('Footnotes') when absent. The Footnoteitem rule (not mine) supplies the numbered <a> and the per-note <div class="footnote-footer" id="footnote-N">. The one correction in this stage is in the render file: the title is escaped with htmlspecialchars($title, ENT_COMPAT) rather than the one-argument default, so an apostrophe is emitted raw as live emits it while &, <, > and " remain escaped. The parse file was not changed; the parse-and-collect mechanism above is the fossil mechanism confirmed against live.

## Corrections and their classifications
One correction was applied, to ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Footnoteblock.php, classified as a PHP-version artifact: PHP 8.1 changed htmlspecialchars()'s default flags from ENT_COMPAT to ENT_QUOTES|ENT_SUBSTITUTE, so under the harness's PHP 8.5.4 the fossil's one-argument call turned a title apostrophe into &#039; where live emits '. Live probe muse-probe-r2notes-fnb-title-apostrophe settles the raw apostrophe and muse-probe-r2notes-fnb-title-special settles &amp;, &lt;, &gt; and &quot;, and the controlled A/B toggle changed only that apostrophe case. Pre-correction bytes are preserved as Footnoteblock.Render.before.php (SHA-256 431444bd7ad8df8393d668730f05835729e1626778e2fb0bd933e43237b649b3) and, for completeness although it was not edited, Footnoteblock.Parse.before.php (SHA-256 2a07940a8403da284259073c3b01d677529738fbec78855f19b44539c89d46f2). No correction was classified as a fossil-era artifact, a reference bug or a harness matter, and no other file was touched.

## Battery and probe numbers
battery.jsonl holds 48 whole documents; ref-php/harness/batch.php produced 48/48 render records with zero errors before and zero after, and 48/48 token records before and after, while the shared ftml dumper at HEAD 6a029bf416651e0d68408f7c977b4cae30ebec55 produced 48/48 records with 14 carrying ftml parse errors on deliberately malformed or multiple-block forms. Twenty live probe pages were created on sandbox-for-codex under muse-probe-r2notes-fnb-, saved, fetched twice anonymously and read back; every one has stable=True, saved_source_matches=True and valid=True, and the cached page HTML, source and sha256 values are in probe-captures.json with the page list in probe-pages.txt. The initial one-shot capture hit a transient ConnectTimeout and succeeded when split into four paged chunks. Byte-for-byte agreement of the whole render body against ftml, normalizing only &#39; to ', is 0/48 because the newline surface differs everywhere; on the isolated footer element, normalizing quoting, inter-tag whitespace and <br />, structurally equal footers went from 39/46 before the correction to 41/46 after it. Against live, the corrected reference footer matches structurally in 15 of 16 probe cases with a footer on all three sides, and ftml matches in 16 of 16; ftml's own apostrophe title match is the case the reference previously got wrong.

## Remaining disagreements and dispositions
F1 is the known frame-level quoting surface: ftml serializes the footnote onclick apostrophe as &#39; and the reference and live use a raw quote; this is a candidate ftml divergence for the coordinator and was not fixed in the reference. F2 is the empty quote/list container: live and ftml drop an empty quote or list line while the reference renders <blockquote></blockquote> or <ul><li></li></ul>, and a control with no footnoteblock in the line shows the divergence belongs to the Blockquote and List rules, so it is routed to lead rather than edited here. F3 is the inline/tight block: the reference leaves a stray </p> inside the footer div while live and ftml keep the footer well-formed, owned by the Paragraph rule and routed to lead. F4 is the newline/pretty-print surface around the footer, a serving-layer/frame-level difference owned by later rules, recorded as informational. F5 is note-body <br /> versus <br> and paragraph spacing, a Footnoteitem/Paragraph frame-level and candidate ftml surface. F6 and F7 record agreement: multiple blocks, attribute handling, empty titles, case variants and malformed forms match live across all three sides. F8 is the corrected title escaping. The detailed evidence and numbering are in findings-for-root.txt and compare-report.md. A question arose during the run about whether the before/after captures were comparable given concurrent sibling edits to Footnoteitem, Bibliography and Bibitem; the assumption proceeded on was to isolate this rule with a controlled toggle of only the render file and to compare footers structurally against live, and that limitation is disclosed in both reports.
