Scope and method. These are the seams the six per-rule batteries cannot own: footnote numbering inside other constructs, the footnoteblock's position relative to the notes it collects, bibliography ordering, citations to entries defined later, duplicate bibitem labels, and the footnote and bibliography families in one document. The battery is conformance-refphp/notes/xmake_battery.py and xbattery.jsonl (39 whole documents); the reference ran through batch.php in render and tokens modes (x-ref-render-pre.jsonl, x-ref-tokens-pre.jsonl), the shared ftml dumper at the recorded HEAD 6a029bf41 produced x-ftml-dump-pre.jsonl, all with empty stderr. For the live comparison 22 focused sources (probe-x-all.jsonl) went to 20 untagged pages and 2 tagged pages under muse-probe-r2notes-x-, captured twice anonymously into captures-x.json. All 22 probes are valid, stable and source-matching. x-ref-render-probe.jsonl and x-ftml-dump-probe.jsonl are the reference and dumper runs on exactly those no-trailing-newline sources. compare_xprobe.py extracts a family-shape signature (ordered footnote reference numbers, collected item numbers, footer count and title, bibliography entry numbers, bibcite target numbers, undefined-label errors and literal unmatched forms) and reports the three-way result in x-probe-compare.txt.

Settled result. All 22 valid probes agree on the family-shape signature on all three sides: 22 ALL3, 0 reference-plus-live, 0 ftml-plus-live, 0 reference-plus-ftml-only, 0 all-disagree. Under whole-string canonicalization that also strips the &#39; entity, the bibcite id hash suffix, frame whitespace and the <br>/<br /> split, 20 of 22 probes agree on all three strings. The two exceptions, x-fn-in-collapsible and x-bib-unterminated, are non-ftml residuals: the reference's Collapsible render emits the older id-based folded/unfolded markup and its Deflist render indents <dt>/<dd> and wraps the block in <p>, where live and ftml both emit the class-based collapsible markup and the flat definition list. X1: footnote numbering inside other constructs. Live numbers notes in document order across divs, collapsibles, blockquotes and list items, and a note inside a true Iftags body is numbered while one inside a suppressed body is dropped without consuming a number (x-fn-in-div, -collapsible, -quote, -list, -iftags-keep, -iftags-drop; the tagged probes use tags=["alpha"] and are captured against pages published with that tag). Reference and ftml agree on all six family shapes. A note inside a [[code]] span stays literal and does not consume a number on all three sides (x-fn-in-code).

X2: footnoteblock position. Live, reference and ftml all collect every note in the document into the first block regardless of where it sits: before the notes (x-fn-block-before), between them (x-fn-block-between), after them (x-fn-block-after in the battery), with no block at all (x-fn-block-none, where the fossil's end-of-string alternative in Footnoteblock::parse emits the collection at document end), with a custom title (x-fn-block-title) and with multiple blocks (x-fn-block-multiple, where only the first [[footnoteblock]] is consumed and a later one stays literal). An empty block with no notes renders nothing on all three sides.

X3: bibliography ordering and resolution. Live preserves definition order for entries regardless of label alphabetical order (x-bib-def-order; x-bib-unordered-labels has zeta, alpha, mu numbered 1, 2, 3), resolves citations by label to those numbers, resolves citations that appear before the bibliography (x-bib-cite-before), resolves a duplicate label to the last definition and numbers both entries (x-bib-dup-label, citation -> 2), and emits the inline "Bibliography item not found" span for an undefined label (x-bib-undefined). Reference and ftml agree on all five family shapes. This matches ftml's dedicated bibliography-order model, where Bibliography is live rule position 23 and Bibcite runs after the body join.

X4: the two families in one document. Live, reference and ftml agree when a footnote and a bibliography citation share a paragraph (x-mix-fn-bib), when a footnote contains a bibcite and is rendered inside the collected block (x-mix-bibcite-in-fn), and when a bibliography entry contains a footnote that is numbered and collected (x-mix-fn-in-bib). Malformed and unterminated forms also agree at family shape: an unterminated [[footnote]] stays literal with no collected note (x-fn-unterminated), and an unterminated [[bibliography]] stays literal while its : k : line becomes a definition list (x-bib-unterminated).

X5: included content. A focused two-page probe under the same prefix settles the numbering seam: muse-probe-r2notes-x-inc-src holds "included [[footnote]]from include[[/footnote]] text" and muse-probe-r2notes-x-inc-holder holds a local note, the include and a footnoteblock. Live numbers the local note 1 and the included note 2 and collects both (captures-x-inc.json, both pages valid, stable and source-matching). The reference matches exactly once the include target is added to the harness page map; the shared ftml dumper has no includer by design and leaves the include literal, which is a serving-layer boundary of the dumper rather than a family divergence. The page-map addition is disclosed in R2-NOTES.md.
