F1 (candidate ftml divergence; assign an M-number continuing from R2-LINK.md). In ftml's legacy Wikidot layout, HtmlContext escapes the single quotes of both the footnote back-reference onclick in render_footnote_block (src/render/html/element/footnotes.rs, the "footnoteref-{index}" onclick) and the superscript reference onclick in render_footnote to &#39;, so the footnote item renders as <a href="javascript:;" onclick="WIKIDOT.page.utils.scrollToReference(&#39;footnoteref-1&#39;)">1</a>. Live Wikidot and the corrected PHP reference both emit the raw quote <a href="javascript:;" onclick="WIKIDOT.page.utils.scrollToReference('footnoteref-1')">1</a>. This is settled byte-for-byte by 25 footnote-footer back-references across the 21 live probes that carry a footer (raw quote in 25 of 25, entity in 0 of 25), by probes muse-probe-r2notes-fni-p-one, -p-two, -p-empty, -p-block-first and -p-div-first, and by all 50 battery cases against conformance-refphp/notes/rust-dump/target/release/r2notes-rust-dump. ftml's own derived wikidot.html pins also contain &#39;, so the entity is intrinsic to its renderer, not a dumper artifact; the ParityTests.md note that wikidot.html is derived FTML output means the pin is not itself live evidence, which is why the live probes were taken. Live==ftml footer-list raw agreement is 0 of 21 and rises only to 18 of 21 after canonicalizing &#39; to a raw quote. I did not fix ftml and did not change the reference to match ftml: the reference already agrees with live here, and the brief classifies this as frame-level surface owned by later rules / a candidate ftml divergence for the coordinator.

F2 (frame-level surface owned by later rules, with a footnote-block tier decision; route to the Footnoteblock owner and the integration pass rather than to Footnoteitem). When a collected note body begins with a block element, live puts a newline between the back-reference dot and the body, while the reference puts a space; the same family of differences includes list indentation and trailing newline placement inside the footer. Live probe muse-probe-r2notes-fni-p-div-first renders <a ...>1</a>.\n<div>\n<p>block body</p>\n</div> whereas the reference renders <a ...>1</a>. <div ><p>block body</p>\n\n</div> and ftml renders <a ...>1</a>.\n<div><p>block body</p></div>; probe -p-list-body renders <a ...>1</a>. * one\n<ul>\n<li>two</li>\n</ul>\n</div> live versus the reference's four-space <li> indentation and doubled trailing newlines. The newline-versus-space decision is made in ftml's render_footnote_block through its tier logic (wikidot_footnote_block / render_wikidot_footnote_tier), while the <div > double space, the <li> indentation and the trailing-newline padding are Div, List and Paragraph serializer surface. These two probes are the only live-vs-reference footer disagreements remaining after my correction (live==reference footer-list raw 19 of 21, up from 17 of 21). I did not fix them in Footnoteitem.php because the Footnoteitem render token carries only {type, id} and has no access to the parsed shape of the note body; a raw-wikitext heuristic would be unsound, and the block-level markup itself belongs to other rules' files. Battery cases fni-12-paragraphs, fni-13-list-body, fni-14-blockquote-body and fni-15-div-body reproduce the same family locally against ftml.
