# R2-notes Footnoteitem rule: differential, correction, and section (2026-11-20)

Method. Footnoteitem has regex=null and no source match of its own: Footnoteblock emits a start/end Footnoteitem token pair around each collected note, and the Xhtml render of those tokens produces the `<div class="footnote-footer" id="footnote-N">` wrapper, the back-reference `<a>` and the `.` separator, plus the closing `</div>`. Every differential is therefore a whole document. I compared three sides over a 50-case whole-document battery and a 22-case live probe set. The reference side is the shared read-only harness ref-php/harness/batch.php in modes render and tokens, run with /usr/bin/php into ref-render-before.jsonl, ref-tokens-before.jsonl and their probe and -after counterparts, with empty stderr sidecars. The ftml side is the one shared dumper at conformance-refphp/notes/rust-dump/target/release/r2notes-rust-dump at the read-only checkout HEAD 6a029bf416651e0d68408f7c977b4cae30ebec55 recorded in that crate's FTML-HEAD.txt, producing ftml-dump.jsonl and ftml-probe-dump.jsonl with empty stderr. The live side is 22 saved anonymous page views on sandbox-for-codex.wikidot.com under prefix muse-probe-r2notes-fni-, created and captured with the bundled wikidot-probe CLI through scripts/wikidot-python (probe-cases.jsonl, probe-map.json, captures.json and the probe-create/probe-capture logs); all 22 captures are stable across two independent anonymous GETs, source-matching by sha256, and valid, and the probe pages are left in place and listed by name in the map. Sources are LF-only with trailing newlines stripped because Wikidot normalizes them on save. Evidence order when sides disagreed was ftml corpus pins, then frozen captures, then live probes, with live winning every shape it settled; I read the ftml parse and render implementation in src/render/html/element/footnotes.rs and src/parsing/rule/impls/block/blocks/footnote.rs and the footnote corpus pins, while treating those pins as hypotheses only, since ParityTests.md states that a tree-test wikidot.html is derived FTML output and must not be cited as live evidence for itself. The battery is reproducible with make_battery.py, the probes with make_probes.py, the reference snapshots by swapping Footnoteitem.render.before.php into place and back, and the reports with compare.py, compare_live.py, compare-report.txt and findings-for-root.txt in this directory.

## Mechanism as corrected

Parse rule (ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Footnoteitem.php). The file is byte-identical to its pre-correction state (SHA-256 6360b50d6349074f7cf8e10f37c2af4d160dc9fd7250f3aa6302cbd54c1a2770, preserved as Footnoteitem.parse.before.php) and was not edited. Its `regex` is null and its process method is a vestigial token-add helper that the pipeline never reaches by source match; no live-settled shape justifies changing it. Footnote::process still collects the trimmed note body into `vars['footnotes'][id-1]`, and Footnoteblock::process still walks that array to emit the start/end Footnoteitem tokens with `{type, id}` options before and after the content string. Numbering, collection, block placement and options remain the properties of the Footnote and Footnoteblock rules and were confirmed unchanged across all three sides.

Render rule (ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Footnoteitem.php). This is the only file I changed (pre-correction SHA-256 7297c549ca82b008f4844ec5adcc447a3f80221f83de32310c1923088c095e57 preserved as Footnoteitem.render.before.php; corrected SHA-256 74ad6ffb1941b78216f9b9fea7a1e51fa13c32ea6957d05919d635ab43b81f06). For `type=start` it emits `<div class="footnote-footer" id="footnote-<prefix><id>">` followed by `<a href="javascript:;" onclick="WIKIDOT.page.utils.scrollToReference('footnoteref-<prefix><id>')"><id></a>.`, then a single separator space only when the collected note body at `vars['footnotes'][id-1]` is non-empty; for `type=end` it emits `</div>`. The id_prefix render configuration is unset in the shared harness on both sides, so the ids are `footnote-1`, `footnoteref-1` and so on, exactly as on live. The back-reference `<a>` has no id and no class, matching live and ftml. The footer bodies themselves are rendered by the other family and frame rules after the start token, so this rule owns the wrapper, the back-reference, the dot and the empty-versus-non-empty separator space, and nothing else.

## Corrections made

I made exactly one correction, in the Footnoteitem Xhtml render rule, and classify it as a fossil-era artifact (a reference bug against live): the fossil appended the `. ` separator space unconditionally, so an empty or whitespace-only collected note rendered `<a ...>1</a>. </div>` with a stray trailing space, while live renders `<a ...>1</a>.</div>` with no space. This is live-settled rather than an ftml-driven change: probe muse-probe-r2notes-fni-p-empty (one empty note followed by a plain note) and probe -p-empty-ws (a whitespace-only body) both show the no-space form in the cached saved-page HTML, and Footnote::process trims the stored body so both cases reach the render as the empty string. The correction reads the already-collected note body from `vars['footnotes']`, the same document state the Footnoteblock render reads, and emits the space only for a non-empty body; probe -p-comment-body confirms the converse, that a stored body which renders literal `[[comment]]...[[/comment]]` text is non-empty and correctly keeps the space. No PHP-version artifact or harness fix was involved, and I did not edit any shared harness or ftml file. I explicitly did not correct the onclick quote spelling, because on 25 of 25 live back-references live emits the raw single quote that the reference already emits and ftml is the side that differs; that divergence is routed as candidate finding F1 rather than treated as a reference error.

## Battery and probe numbers

The 50-case battery covers one to four notes per document, empty and whitespace-only bodies, nested bold/italic/mono, links, multiline and multi-paragraph bodies, lists, blockquotes, divs, code, HTML-special characters and Unicode inside note bodies, nested footnotes, block positions before, between and after the notes, two blocks, no block, an empty block, block options (title, empty and special-character title, hide, unquoted title), unterminated and stray note tags, fused openers, a malformed block opener, and adjacency with Bibitem, Bibliography, Bibcite, Div, Blockquote, List and Collapsible. The 22 live probes settle the quote spelling, the empty-body separator, numbering across block positions, a comment-only body, a block-first body, nested footnotes, list and quote bodies, a citation adjacent to a footnote, and an unterminated note. Against the corrected reference the battery reaches whole-string raw agreement with ftml of 1 of 50 and entity/newline-canonical agreement of 6 of 50, with 43 of 50 cases carrying footers on both sides; footnote-footer-list raw agreement with ftml is 7 of 50 before and after (the raw quote difference persists on every footer) and entity/newline-canonical agreement rises from 42 of 50 before to 46 of 50 after. On the 21 probes that carry a footer, live==reference footer-list raw agreement rises from 17 of 21 before the correction to 19 of 21 after; live==ftml raw agreement is 0 of 21 and becomes 18 of 21 after canonicalizing `&#39;` to a raw quote. The live questions settle cleanly: 25 of 25 live back-references use the raw quote and 0 use the entity, and 0 of 2 empty-body footers use a trailing space while 2 of 2 omit it. Before and after token snapshots are byte-identical in both battery and probe modes (this is a render-only correction), every stderr sidecar is empty, and the controlled before/after render diff touches exactly fni-05, fni-06, fni-07 and fni-08 in the battery and p-empty and p-empty-ws in the probes. All 22 probes are valid, stable and source-matching, and none was dropped or padded.

## Remaining disagreements, dispositions, and candidate findings

Class S (frame-level surface owned by later rules, not a Footnoteitem divergence). The two remaining live-vs-reference probe footer disagreements are p-div-first and p-list-body: with a block-first body live puts a newline after the back-reference dot where the reference puts a space, and live's list inside a footer uses no four-space `<li>` indentation and a single trailing newline where the reference pads. The newline-versus-space decision is ftml's render_footnote_block tier logic (wikidot_footnote_block and render_wikidot_footnote_tier), while `<div >` with a double space, the `<li>` indentation, the `<br>` versus `<br />` spelling and the trailing paragraph newlines are Div, List, Paragraph and Typography serializer surface. The reference's Footnoteitem render token carries only `{type, id}` and cannot see the parsed shape of the note body, so a correct fix cannot be made in this file without an unsound raw-wikitext heuristic; these are routed to the Footnoteblock owner and the integration pass as finding F2 rather than corrected here. The reference's trailing `\n\n` after a paragraph, its `<div >` spacing and the newline padding inside div and blockquote wrappers are the same already-recorded later-rule surface.

Class T (serving-layer scope). Page and site state is evaluated only as far as the anonymous saved-page view makes it observable and deterministic; real image or math rendering inside a note body, user-specific content and anything requiring an authenticated page view remain outside this rule. Note numbering inside suppressed Iftags bodies and included content is root's cross-rule integration and was noted rather than owned here.

Candidate ftml divergences, numbered for the coordinator. F1: ftml's legacy Wikidot layout serializes the footnote back-reference and superscript onclick single quotes as `&#39;` where live Wikidot and the reference emit the raw quote; 25 of 25 live footers settle this against 0 of 25 with the entity, and ftml's derived wikidot.html pins carry the entity intrinsically. Assign an M-number continuing from R2-LINK.md and route to the coordinator; I did not edit ftml. F2: block-first and list bodies inside a footnote footer differ in separator and block markup placement between live, ftml and the reference, with live and ftml agreeing on the newline and the reference emitting a space; route to the Footnoteblock owner and integration, with the frame-level components noted above. F3 and F4 in findings-for-root.txt are a harness-matter disclosure and confirmations that need no correction, respectively, and are not candidate ftml divergences.

Coverage gaps and a recorded question. I probed an empty body, a whitespace-only body and a comment-only body, but not every construct that can render to zero visible text, so the separator rule is stated in terms of the trimmed stored body and any future zero-visible-text construct that live treats as empty would need its own probe. The question that arose mid-work was whether the empty-body trailing space was a Footnoteitem artifact or a global page-level trailing-whitespace trim; I proceeded on the Footnoteitem interpretation because the space appears mid-document in probe p-empty (empty note first, plain note second) and cannot be explained by an end-of-page trim, and live confirms the no-space form there. Any future probe of these surfaces should reuse the cached captures rather than fetching the pages again.
