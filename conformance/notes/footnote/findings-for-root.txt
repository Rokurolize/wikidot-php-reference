Candidate findings for the coordinator, R2-notes Footnote rule. Neither is a reference bug and neither was "fixed"; both are routed for the coordinator's M-style consolidated ftml findings list.

FN-1 (frame-level surface, anticipated by the brief). ftml's HTML serializer writes the single quote inside the footnoteref anchor's onclick attribute as &#39;, while live Wikidot and the reference emit a raw '. Evidence: live saved page muse-probe-r2notes-fn-quote-encoding, captured HTML contains onclick="WIKIDOT.page.utils.scrollToReference('footnote-1')" with raw quotes, valid/stable/source-match all true; the same source's reference render is byte-identical on that anchor; ftml dump and the ftml pin test/footnote/basic/wikidot.html contain &#39;. Impact: 46 of 53 battery cases differ raw on the anchor sequence, 3 of 53 are identical raw, and 52 of 53 are identical after normalizing &#39; to '. Disposition: frame-level serialization surface owned by later rules, candidate only, not a correction to ref-php/text-wiki/Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Footnote.php.

FN-2 (candidate ftml divergence). ftml accepts a mixed-case closing tag [[/FootNote]] as the closer of a lowercase [[footnote]] opener, while live Wikidot and the reference require the exact lowercase [[/footnote]]. Evidence: live probe muse-probe-r2notes-fn-case-close (source A[[footnote]]two[[/FootNote]] B[[footnote]]three[[/footnote]]) renders exactly one footnoteref anchor whose body spans to the later lowercase closer; the ftml dumper renders two anchors, treating [[/FootNote]] as a closer. Live probe muse-probe-r2notes-fn-mixed-case (source A[[FootNote]]mix[[/footnote]] B[[footnote]]two[[/FootNote]]) renders zero anchors, matching the reference, while ftml renders one. Battery case fn17-mixed-case-tag shows the same reference-zero / ftml-one split. Also settled and agreeing: mixed-case and uppercase openers ([[FootNote]]/[[FOOTNOTE]]) are literal on both live and the reference, so no opener-case finding. Disposition: candidate ftml divergence in its legacy block closer name matching; live behavior wins, the reference is correct, and ftml was not modified.

Routed observations that are not findings for this rule: auto-appended footnoteblock when footnotes exist and no block is authored is present on both live and the reference (verified by fn01-single and the live quote-encoding probe), and the footer markup, empty-item spacing, <br /> versus <br>, inter-block blank lines and trailing document newlines are frame-level or later-rule surface outside Footnote.php's two files.
