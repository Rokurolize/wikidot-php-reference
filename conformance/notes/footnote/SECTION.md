# Part 1: Footnote

Mechanism as corrected. No correction was required, so the mechanism is the fossil mechanism confirmed against live. The parse rule matches `[[footnote]]`...`[[/footnote]]` with the case-sensitive dot-all regular expression `;\s*\[\[footnote\]\](.*?)\[\[/footnote\]\];s`, consumes leading whitespace before the opener, trims the captured body, appends it to `Text_Wiki::$vars['footnotes']` at index `id-1` where `id` is `count(vars['footnotes'])+1`, and emits a Footnote token carrying only that sequential id. The render rule builds the superscript anchor `<sup class="footnoteref"><a id="footnoteref-{prefix}{id}" href="javascript:;" class="footnoteref" onclick="WIKIDOT.page.utils.scrollToReference('footnote-{prefix}{id}')">{id}</a></sup>`; `getConf('id_prefix')` is unset in the reference harness, so the prefix is empty and ids are `footnoteref-1`, `footnote-1` and so on, exactly as live Wikidot renders them. The two unused locals in the render rule (`$label` from a token key the parser never sets, and `$refs` reading Math's static equation map) are harmless fossil-era residue with no observable effect and were left untouched. The harness's per-case reset of `vars['footnotes']`, `vars['bibitems']` and `vars['bibitemIds']` is root-owned and behaviour-neutral for this rule; numbering restarts at 1 per document on both sides.

Corrections and classifications. None. The owned files are byte-identical to their pre-correction state; the preserved copies and digests are `Footnote.Parse.before.php` (SHA-256 8e076978cb60591682968dcd9179a48a054408d489dacf14b81b0676360440ab) and `Footnote.Render.before.php` (SHA-256 cb59b7cfbc0e877b32c01704d370f5cd2e9f73708f35e453c350562a751968f2), also recorded in `before-sha256.txt`. No correction class applies (no fossil-era artifact, no reference bug, no PHP version artifact, no harness matter) because no live-settled shape diverges from the fossil on the surface this rule owns. In particular, mixed-case openers and closers were not "fixed" to match ftml: live probes settled that the reference's case-sensitive behaviour is correct. Because no owned file changed, `ref-render-after.jsonl` and `ref-tokens-after.jsonl` are not produced; the before captures are the operative reference output, and an after capture would only be meaningful if this rule's bytes had changed.

Battery numbers. `battery.jsonl` holds 53 whole-document cases (generator `make_battery.py`), covering single, multiple and ten-note documents, empty and whitespace-only bodies, bold/italic/underline, links, code, math, lists, quotes, multiline and Unicode bodies, block before/between/after/duplicate/absent, title/hide/bogus/empty-title block options, unterminated, stray-closer, nested, fused-close, underscore-closer, space-opener, mixed-case and uppercase tags, div/list/quote/collapsible/iftags wrapping, table and header adjacency, and adjacency with bibitem, bibliography, bibcite and footnoteitem in pipeline order. The reference harness produced 53 render records and 53 token records and the shared ftml dumper produced 53 records, all with exit code 0 and empty stderr. 49 of 53 documents emit at least one footnoteref anchor and 49 of 53 token streams contain a Footnote token; ftml reports parse errors in 18 cases, all deliberately malformed or adjacent shapes. Comparing the exact footnoteref-anchor sequences, raw agreement is 3/53 and agreement after normalizing the known frame-level `&#39;` to a raw quote is 52/53; the only normalized mismatch is `fn17-mixed-case-tag` (reference 0 anchors, ftml 1). Full numbers and the per-case normalized diff are in `compare-report.txt` and `compare-report-anchors.txt`.

Probe numbers. Twelve run-owned pages were created on sandbox-for-codex under the disjoint prefix `muse-probe-r2notes-fn-` and captured through the bundled `wikidot-probe` via `scripts/wikidot-python`: `muse-probe-r2notes-fn-case-close`, `-case-open`, `-mixed-case`, `-uppercase`, `-numbering`, `-unterminated`, `-empty-body`, `-nested`, `-fused-close`, `-space-opener`, `-quote-encoding` and `-block-title` (fullname/source map in `probe-map.json`, sources in `probe-cases.jsonl`). Every capture is `valid=true`, `stable=true` (two independent anonymous GETs identical) and `saved_source_matches=true`; the JSON captures are cached once in `probe-captures.json` and reused for all comparisons, alongside cached `probe-ref-render.jsonl` and `probe-ftml-dump.jsonl`. The reference footnoteref-anchor sequence matches the live saved-page sequence exactly, with raw quotes, in 12/12 probes; ftml matches live after `&#39;` normalization in 10/12 and differs in `case-close` and `mixed-case`. The pages are left in place as required and are listed in the evidence.

Remaining disagreements and dispositions. The `&#39;` versus raw quote in the onclick attribute is a frame-level serialization surface owned by later rules and is routed as candidate finding FN-1; live emits the raw quote, so the reference is correct and was not changed. ftml's acceptance of `[[/FootNote]]` as a closer for a lowercase `[[footnote]]`, where live and the reference require the exact lowercase closer, is routed as candidate ftml divergence FN-2 with the case-close, mixed-case and `fn17-mixed-case-tag` evidence; live wins and ftml was not modified. Footer markup, empty-item spacing, `<br />` versus `<br>`, inter-block blank lines and trailing document newlines are frame-level or later-rule surface outside this rule's two files and are not corrected here. No serving-layer question arises for this rule: matching, numbering and the anchor display shape are fully observable and deterministic in the anonymous saved-page view. Both findings are itemized with evidence in `findings-for-root.txt`.

Questions and assumptions. A question arose about whether after captures should be produced even with no file change; the assumption proceeded on is that they should not, because other family owners are editing their own rule files concurrently in the shared worktree and a re-run would conflate their changes with this rule. A second question was whether ftml's case-insensitive closer reflected live; the live probes settled it as an ftml divergence rather than a reference defect, so no assumption was needed.
