## Part 2: Module (conformance-refphp/module/module/SECTION.md, COMPLETE)

Module matches [[module Name attr]] openers at a line start with case-insensitive [a-z0-9_\-\/]+ names, optional raw attributes, zero or more spaces then a newline after the opener, and an optional body closed by [[/module]], looping preg_replace_callback, defending against self-nesting by rewriting a multi-opener match to [[module654, and emitting trimmed moduleName, attr and body options. The 52-case battery shows 17 outputs changed by the corrections; after correction 17 cases agree exactly with ftml under frame normalization, 21 are serving-layer marker surface, 5 are the name-charset issue and 9 are body/closer/cross-rule cases, with no errors on either side.
