# R2-List Part 2: Deflist

Mechanism as corrected. Deflist is the thirty-fourth pipeline rule, running after List and before Table. Its fossil parse rule matches a run of lines that begin with ": " through the regex `/\n((: ).*\n)(?!(: |\n))/Us`, splits each line on the first whitespace-colon-whitespace separator with `/^(: )(.*)?( : )(.*)?$/Ums`, and emits list_start, term_start, term_end, narr_start, narr_end and list_end tokens that the Xhtml render turns into dl, dt and dd tags. Live Wikidot and ftml use the same colon-led term form ": term : definition"; the brief's "; term : definition" hypothesis is wrong and live probe p07-semicolon renders that shape as literal paragraph text, as battery cases def19 and def20 also show. A colon-led line without the separator is a residue line that vanishes rather than a definition-only continuation, settled by live probe p17-def-only-cont, battery case def30 and the ftml pin test/definition-list/missing-separator. Prefilter pads the source with a leading and a trailing newline and expands tabs to four spaces before Deflist runs, which is why ":\t: value" reaches the rule as an empty-term line. Three corrections landed in the two owned files. C1 removes the fossil's four- and eight-space indentation from the dt and dd tokens and changes list_end from "</dl>\n\n" to "</dl>\n"; live probes p01-basic and p02-multi and the ftml pin test/definition-list/basic settle the unindented, single-trailing-newline shape. C2 makes parse return a single line break with no tokens when the matched run contains no definition items, so ": Term :", ": term" and ": " no longer emit an empty <dl></dl>; live probes p04-empty-def and p06-missing-sep and the pins test/definition-list/empty-definition and test/definition-list/missing-separator settle it. C3 suppresses the term_start and term_end pair when the term renders empty, so an empty term yields <dl><dd>Value</dd></dl> rather than an empty <dt></dt>; live probes p03-empty-term, p15-comment-term and p16-tab-prefix, ftml's render test wikidot_definition_list_omits_an_empty_term_tag, the pin test/definition-list/colon-led-tab-prefix and root's routed x35 battery case settle it. For emptiness testing, earlier-rule token placeholders such as a Comment are stripped before trimming, because they have already replaced the visible source by the time Deflist runs.

Remaining disagreements and dispositions. No candidate ftml divergence arose in the Deflist matching space, so the DEF-F sequence is empty and this rule contributes nothing to the stage's M-style ftml findings list. ROUTE-1 is Paragraph-rule surface: live and ftml leave prose adjacent to a definition list unwrapped while the reference wraps it in <p>...</p>, seen in p12-prose-adjacent, p09-residue and battery def04, def06, def24, def31, def33, def44 and def50. ROUTE-2 is Strong-rule surface later in the pipeline: a bold span crossing two definition lines closes at each item end on live and in ftml but stays open across the generated dt and dd tags in the reference, seen in p10-bold-cross, battery def40 and ftml's own pin test/list/definition-scope-cross; inserting a raw line break in Deflist would be re-tokenized by the later Newline rule into an unwanted <br />, so the fix belongs to Strong's item-scope handling and was not attempted here. ROUTE-3 is Div-rule surface where the reference emits <div > with a stray space, seen in p14-in-div and def37. ROUTE-4 is Code, Blockquote and List pretty-print and inter-element whitespace surface seen in def32, def36, def39 and def44. ROUTE-5 is bibliography-family surface in deflist-shaped bodies: the bibcite id suffix is serving-layer noise, the &#39; entity is the already routed M28, and bibliography div newline layout is the NOTES stage's BIB-F2; the definition and bibitem shapes themselves agree on all three sides in p11-bib and def45 through def59. The <br /> versus <br> spelling and a trailing newline after </p> are frame-level surfaces throughout. The ROUTE items are written out in findings-for-root.txt.

Questions and assumptions carried forward. The brief's semicolon form was treated as a hypothesis and settled by live and pins as literal text, and both that shape and the real colon-led shape are in the battery. The phrase "definition-only continuation form" was read as a colon-led line lacking the whitespace-colon-whitespace separator, which live and pins show vanishes rather than becoming a dd. The empty-term test strips all delimiter-wrapped token placeholders before trimming, which is correct for the live-settled comment-only and tab-prefix shapes; a term made solely of a token that renders non-empty text would be misclassified, but no live-settled shape exercises that and it is recorded as an assumption rather than silently changed. Live cannot stabilize a trailing-space source, so those two shapes rely on frozen pins rather than probes. The ftml checkout was read only, no cargo was run, no git commit or git identity variable was used, and the shared harness and dumper were used read-only.
