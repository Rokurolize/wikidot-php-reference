What live Wikidot does. The Anchor rule owns two forms at pipeline position 24 (after `Freelink` and `Raw`, before `Div`, `Newline`, `Paragraph`, `Url`, `Superscript`, `Underline`, and `Typography`):
1. Named anchors `[[# <name>]]`: requires `[[#` followed by a horizontal space (` ` or `\t`) and a non-empty single-line name before `]]`. When `<name>` consists solely of `[-_A-Za-z0-9.%]`, live emits `<a name="<name>"></a>` (leaving a third `]` outside on `]]]`). When `<name>` is non-empty and contains characters outside that set (such as internal or extra leading/trailing spaces or `$`), live emits the single-bracketed pseudo-link `[<leading-ws><a href="javascript:;"><trimmed-name></a><trailing-ws>]`, except when closed by a non-triple-link residual `]]]` (`[[# apple banana]]]`), which stays literal. `[[#durian]]` (no space after `#`) and `[[# ]]` (empty name after space) stay literal.
2. Anchor elements `[[a ...]]...[[/a]]` and scored `[[a_ ...]]...[[/a]]`: tag matching is ASCII case-insensitive (`[[a]]`, `[[A]]`, `[[a_]]`, `[[A_]]`, closed by `[[/a]]` or `[[/A]]`), while `[[anchor]]...[[/anchor]]`, `[[*a]]...[[/a]]`, `[[a]]...[[/anchor]]`, and a space-only opener argument region (`[[a ]]...[[/a]]`) stay literal (`p09`, `test/anchor/space-only-argument-region`). Attributes are parsed by `Text_Wiki_Parse::getAttrs` (exact `key="value"` syntax; single-quoted, unquoted, or spaced `=` forms are not separate attributes, settled by `p15`, and `"abc="` yields an empty `href`, settled by `test/anchor/href-scheme-normalized`). Attribute keys are case-sensitive lowercase except `data-*`, where the `data-` prefix must be lowercase and the suffix is lowercased (`data-X` -> `data-x`, while `DATA-X`, `CLASS`, `HREF` are dropped). The allowlist is `id` (isolated with `u-`, empty omitted), `class`, `style`, `role`, `type`, `data-*` (in authored order, empty `data-x=""` preserved), `href` (always emitted, defaulting to `href=""`), and `target`, emitted in that exact order (`test/anchor/attribute-allowlist`). Values strip non-whitespace C0 controls and DEL, collapse ASCII whitespace runs to a single space, and trim both ends; an attribute value containing a Raw token delimiter (`\xFF`) normalizes to `""` on `class`/`data-*`/verbatim `href` and to the token digits on non-verbatim `href` (`test/anchor/raw-attribute-value`). For `href`, an optional leading `*` is stripped; if the remainder starts with a verbatim prefix (`#`, `/`, `http://`, `https://`, `ftp://`, `mailto:`, `gopher://`, `news://`, `mms://`, `git://`, `sftp://`, `ssh://`, `svn://`, `telnet://`, `cvs://`), spaces are encoded as `%20`, non-ASCII bytes are uppercase percent-encoded (`p12`), and a leading `*` implies `target="_blank"` unless an explicit `target` was authored (`p11`); otherwise the remainder is normalized into a lowercased page slug with `:` or `-` separators and no implied `target="_blank"` (`test/anchor/href-scheme-normalized`, `p11`). Leading and trailing horizontal spaces inside the body are hoisted outside `<a ...>...</a>` when adjacent to non-whitespace on the same line (`test/anchor/boundary-space-moves-out`). A nested `[[a...]]` opener inside an open `[[a...]]` closes the outer `<a>` before opening the inner `<a>` and consumes a matching trailing `[[/a]]` (`test/anchor/nested-open-empty-pair-space`). The scored form `[[a_ ...]]...[[/a]]` strips a single leading/trailing newline inside its body, joins adjacent lines across single or double line breaks (`p05`, `p06`, `p22-anchor-element`), and suppresses the outer `<p>` wrapper when the run ends with the scored anchor (`p03`, `p04`, `p06`, `PG-F1`).
