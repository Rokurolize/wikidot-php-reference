# R2-table root cross-rule interaction battery: design and assumptions

The interaction battery conformance-refphp/table/xbattery.jsonl (generated by xmake_battery.py, 46 whole-document cases) owns the shapes no single rule owner can own. The families are: tables inside divs, collapsibles and blockquotes, including a div-wrapped quote and both table forms inside containers (x01-x09); the simple (||) and advanced ([[table]]) forms adjacent, back to back without a blank line, and nested in either direction including advanced-in-advanced (x10-x17); cell content carrying the span-shaped inline family (span, size, color, bold), footnotes with a footnoteblock, triple-bracket page links and double-bracket URL links whose target contains a pipe (the F-R2-44 neighborhood), raw markup, and div/collapsible blocks inside simple cells and advanced cells (x18-x30); tables interrupted by headings, toc, lists, deflists, hr and code, and tables after headings and lists (x31-x39); and mixed-form boundaries with colspan/rowspan, trailing-empty span cells, aligned headers, empty advanced cells, a malformed advanced opener beside a simple table, and a two-container mixed document (x40-x46). Every source is LF-only with no trailing newline and no line with trailing whitespace, because Wikidot strips trailing whitespace on save and saved_source_matches must hold.

The three sides are compared by conformance-refphp/table/compare_x.py: the reference render before and after the owners' corrections (x-ref-render-pre.jsonl and x-ref-render-final.jsonl), the shared ftml dumper pinned at HEAD a61ae005d9e837171a3d2b91cf8fc2df7a3d8ae8 (x-ftml-dump.jsonl), and cached live saved anonymous page views under the single prefix muse-probe-r2table-x- created and captured by run_xprobes.sh (x-probe-cases.jsonl, x-probe-map.json, captures-x.json, probe-x-create.log, probe-x-capture.log). The comparator uses the campaign-standard probe.norm masking plus local masks for collapsible and tab/footnote ids, and reports both a family-shape signature (table/tr/td/th structure, cell span attributes, cell class attributes, inline span shapes, literal unmatched constructs and visible text) and whole-string canonical agreement after normalizing <br /> spelling, empty div attributes and inter-tag whitespace. Raw pre-correction canonical agreement between the reference and the dumper is 7 of 46, with the 39 differences dominated by the fossil's pretty-print indentation, its `<td >` attribute space and its inter-block newlines rather than by interaction behavior; x24-cell-with-url-pipe is the F-R2-44 surface already routed by the Block/Toc stage (the reference keeps the pipe inside the URL target, ftml stops at it), and is not a new finding.

Assumptions recorded before the result was known: the harness table css class wiki-content-table is part of the observable shape and both sides are compared with it; frames, footers and page chrome are outside the page-content capture and are not compared; a case whose live capture is invalid, unstable or source-mismatched is dropped rather than adjudicated; and where live, ftml pins and the reference disagree on a shape the two owners do not own, the disposition is stated rather than patched. The checkouts advanced once mid-stage to a module-only commit (no table changes), and all differentials stay at the pinned HEAD recorded in rust-dump/FTML-HEAD.txt.
