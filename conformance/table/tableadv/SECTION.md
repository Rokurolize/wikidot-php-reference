Mechanism as corrected. A document is one advanced table when a `[[table ...]]` opener and its `[[/table]]` closer enclose a body consisting solely of `[[row ...]]` blocks separated by whitespace, and each row body consists solely of `[[cell ...]]` or `[[hcell ...]]` blocks separated by whitespace. A table with no row, a row with no cell, any non-row or non-cell text in those bodies, or an unmatched opener or closer is not a table and stays literal for the other rules. Nested tables are expanded to opaque tokens before the enclosing body is split, so an inner `[[row]]` or `[[cell]]` never leaks into the outer grammar. A table's head tolerates a `]]` pair inside a quoted value, while row and cell heads use the plain token grammar; this reproduces the live split where a quoted `]]` survives on a table head but terminates a row or cell head. Header-ness comes from the closer name, not the opener: `[[cell]]x[[/hcell]]` renders `<th>` and `[[hcell]]x[[/cell]]` renders `<td>`. Attributes are authored `key="value"` pairs filtered through the Wikitext safe-attribute allowlist plus `data-` and `aria-` prefixes, case sensitively at this block level; `title` and `hidden` are dropped, `colspan` and `rowspan` are dropped on tables and rows, a zero span is dropped on cells, `id` values receive the `u-` isolation prefix, values are whitespace-normalized, and the surviving attributes are emitted in sorted key order. Cell bodies keep their inner markup, with a blank-line body wrapped so the Paragraph rule still forms paragraphs, and all whitespace around the structural boundaries is discarded. The renderer emits `<table ...>\n`, `<tr ...>\n`, `<td ...>` or `<th ...>`, a newline after each cell and row close, and a bare `</table>`; advanced tables carry no automatic css class, so the harness's `wiki-content-table` setting applies only to the simple form.

Corrections and classifications. C1, header cells (fossil-era artifact): the fossil cell pattern accepted only `column`, `col` and `cell` and had no `hcell`, so header rows stayed literal and were mangled into freelinks; I added `hcell` and derive the tag from the closer, confirmed by the `advanced` and `advanced-attrs-header-regular` pins and probes adv14, adv15 and adv16. C2, attribute passthrough (reference bug layered on a fossil-era artifact): the fossil copied only `class` and `style`, so ids, data attributes, spans and alignment vanished; I implemented the safe allowlist, case-sensitive filtering, value normalization, `u-` id isolation and sorted serialization, confirmed by the `advanced`, `advanced-colspan-fallback` and `advanced-attrs-header-regular` pins and probes adv26 through adv38. C3, span attributes (fossil-era artifact): the fossil discarded every colspan and rowspan; live passes non-zero spans through, drops a zero span, and rejects spans on tables and rows, confirmed by the `advanced` width section, `advanced-colspan`, `advanced-colspan-fallback` and probes adv19 through adv25. C4, render newline shape (fossil-era artifact): the fossil emitted compact `<table><tr><td>` tags while live and the pins place a newline after each table, row, cell close and row close, so I changed every token render branch. C5, structural validity and aliases (reference bug): the fossil regex admitted `col` and `column` aliases and did not enforce all-row and all-cell bodies, so I added the strict validation and dropped the aliases; `fail`, `advanced` and probes adv09, adv10 and adv56 settle it. C6, quoted brackets on the table head (reference bug): the fossil's `[^\]]*` head stopped at the first `]` inside `class="a]]b"`; the new quoted-aware head reproduces the `quoted-attribute-close-bracket` pin and battery adv32. C7, structural whitespace (fossil-era artifact): the fossil reinserted spaces and newlines between rows and cells as text; I discard them, confirmed by battery adv47. C8, nested-table recursion (reference bug, robustness): nested tables are now tokenized before the outer split, which preserves both the inline and blank-line nested shapes in the `advanced` pin and probes adv48 and adv49. The exact pre-correction bytes are preserved as `Tableadv.php.before.php` and `TableadvXhtml.before.php`; the render copy carries the Xhtml suffix to distinguish it from the parse copy.

Battery and probe numbers. `battery.jsonl` holds 60 whole-document cases generated by `make_battery.py`, covering row and cell counts, multiple tables, inline and midline placement, header rows and mismatched header closers, empty tables, empty rows and empty cells, colspan and rowspan including zero and non-numeric forms, table, row and cell attributes with quoting, unknown keys, upper-case keys, duplicate keys, unquoted keys and id isolation, alignment and style, inline markup, paragraph, list, heading and quote bodies, inline and paragraph nested tables, and literal or interrupted forms including junk in a table or row, unterminated tables and cells, naked rows and cells, the `col` alias, a fused closer, a table in a div, malformed arguments and a quoted bracket in a cell head. Before correction the reference agreed with the dumper on 8 of 60 cases under the stage's frame join normalization and on 0 of 60 byte for byte; after correction it agrees on 50 of 60 under normalization and 40 of 60 byte for byte. All four battery captures have matching empty `.err` sidecars and no case errors. The live set is 20 probe pages under the exclusive prefix `muse-probe-r2table-adv-` on sandbox-for-codex, all 20 valid with two identical anonymous GETs and `saved_source_matches=true`; sources, fullname map, raw captures, both driver logs, the probe-side render and dump files, the adjudication script and its report are under `probes/`. Against live the corrected reference matches 15 of 20 probes and the pinned dumper matches 15 of 20, with the same five cases differing on each side.

Remaining disagreements and dispositions. Ten battery cases still differ from the dumper under normalization and none is a defect in the advanced table rule itself: adv06, adv42, adv43, adv46, adv49, adv55 and adv57 are Paragraph and Blockquote frame-level newlines and paragraph-splitting around block tokens, owned by later rules; adv45 is the Heading rule's treatment of `= Inner =` inside a cell; adv58 is Div's doubled attribute space; and adv59 is a block-pairing seam where a following table absorbs an adjacent empty malformed table, which belongs to the root's cross-rule interactions. Four of the ten have the same shape as live probes rather than a pin mismatch, so they are recorded here rather than treated as silent passes. Serving-layer scope was not otherwise invoked: every table shape was decided from the anonymous saved-page view, and no page or site state feeds this rule. ADV-F1: on probe adv23-colspan-bad the current live page emits `data-name="bad" colspan="abc"` while the frozen pin `test/table/advanced-colspan-fallback/wikidot.html` and the dumper emit `colspan="abc" data-name="bad"`; the attributes and values are identical and only their serialization order differs, so I kept the reference on the pinned order and hand the order question to root with the live capture attached. ADV-F2: on probe adv30-unknown-attrs live drops `aria-label` from a cell while the dumper keeps it, because ftml's safe-attribute list admits the `aria-` prefix; I kept the reference aligned with the pins and flag the prefix discrepancy for root. ADV-F3: on probe adv39-align-center live drops `align="center"` from a cell while the dumper keeps it; the value is a safe attribute in ftml, so I kept the pinned shape and flag the live drift. ADV-F4: on probes adv42 and adv48 live places a single newline around a paragraph or nested table inside a `<td>` while the pins and dumper place none; this is a frame-level surface but it is a live-versus-pin whitespace drift rather than a reference defect, so root should decide whether to route it to the Paragraph rule or accept it as serving-layer formatting. I did not call the reference wrong on any shape that a frozen pin had settled. The `advanced-malformed-arguments` and `table-closer-content-line-in-cell` pins differ from the reference only at the simple-and-advanced or adjacent-table seam, which the root owns.

Questions and assumptions.
