## Mechanism summary

Raw parse rule (Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Raw.php, corrected this stage). The fossil regex was /(?<!@)@@(.*[^@]?)@@/U with no /s: it matched a lazy single-line body between two @@ runs, took $matches[1] verbatim as the token's 'text' option, and stored one Raw token per match. It could not express the live run arithmetic, the cross-line closer, or the empty-raw disappearance. The corrected rule is a left-to-right scanner over the source that mirrors ftml's parsing/rule/impls/raw.rs special cases: @@body@@ is one Raw token with body; @@@@ vanishes; @@@@@ yields a literal @; @@@@@@ yields a literal @@; @@@x@@@ is a Raw token with body @x plus a literal @; @@@@x@@@@ is plain x (two empty raws cancel); @@@@@x@@@@@ is literal @x@; @@\n@@ is a single space; @@x\n@@ is a Raw token with body x; @@x\ny@@ and unterminated @@ stay literal; a closer run of four or more leaves the extra @ as literal text rather than consuming the following opener (live probe raw-adjacent @@one@@@@two@@, ftml pin adjacent-after-close). The scanner leaves the token option shape and rule name unchanged, so all downstream rules and the token dump contract are preserved.

Raw render rule (Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Raw.php, corrected this stage). The fossil returned a bare htmlspecialchars($options['text']), which under this PHP 8 runtime escapes apostrophes as &#039; and neither wraps the body nor preserves spaces as literals. Live (probe raw-basic, raw-ws, raw-entities, and ftml's raw/space-escaping pin) emits <span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">...</span> around the body with Wikidot's raw-escape table: ASCII space becomes &#32;, NUL becomes a regular space, < > & " are entity-escaped, and an apostrophe stays a literal single quote. The corrected renderer applies that table with strtr and adds the wrapper. This is a surface change to the rule's own output, not to the surrounding page; the body itself (markup, brackets, HTML source, entities) stays literal in every case, which is the heart of Raw and is now verified on 39 battery cases.

Rawold parse rule (Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Rawold.php, unchanged). Its regex /(?<!`)``([^']*[^`])``/U matches a double-backtick span whose body contains no apostrophe and ends in a non-backtick, stores the body in a Rawold token's 'text' option, and is applied after Raw, so a `` span inside @@...@@ is never seen by Rawold (the Raw token shields it) and an @@ inside `` is never seen by Raw. The live probes confirm both rules can fire in sequence (old-and-raw) and that apostrophes and empty bodies do not match (old-apostrophe, old-empty), so no parse correction was needed. Rawold render rule (Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Rawold.php, unchanged) returns htmlspecialchars($options['text']) without a pre-wrap span: live rawold bodies keep plain spaces and markup literal while HTML-significant characters are entity-escaped (old-basic, old-wikitext, old-and-raw, plus focused probe oldsemi). The apostrophe-looking entities in the fossil-era cases come from the later Typography rule acting on the backticks left outside an unmatched span, and match live byte-for-byte (old-apostrophe).

Form parse rule (Text/Wiki/Parse/Default/Form.php, corrected this stage). The fossil regex /\[\[form\]\]\s*\n(.*)\n---\s*\n(.*)\n\[\[\/form\]\]/is matched a [[form]] opener line, a YAML definition body, a --- separator line, a data body, and a [[/form]] closer, and then called Wikidot_Form::fromYaml() to emit begin/field/end tokens. On this reference tree that call fataled with Class "Wikidot_Form" not found (12 of 68 battery cases errored before correction), and the fossil snapshot's YAML libraries are empty directories, so the interactive form backend is not reconstructible from the fossil. Live probes settle the observable behavior: on a saved anonymous page view, current live Wikidot renders a matched [[form]] block as literal source text, one raw-style pre-wrap span per line, with the YAML and data bodies untouched, a nbsp/space alternation for leading indentation, and <br /> joins; a block without the --- separator is still claimed (form-no-sep), but an unterminated block is ordinary wikitext (form-unterm, where --- becomes &#8212;-). The corrected parser keeps the block-matching regex but makes the separator optional, stores one Form token per nonblank source line with a 'text' option (and a blank Form token for an empty line so it does not become a paragraph break), and leaves newlines in place for the existing Newline rules. Form render rule (Text/Wiki/Render/Xhtml/Form.php, corrected this stage) emits the same pre-wrap span as Raw, with the leading-space alternation observed live (a leading pair of spaces is &nbsp;&#32;, a leftover single space is &#32;), and nothing for the blank placeholder. The interactive data form and its submission behavior are a serving-layer concern (the loop's MailForm-style disposition): display shape is the spec here, submission effects are not, and nothing in this stage tries to reproduce them.

## Corrections made

C1. Raw wrapper and escape table (fossil-era artifact plus PHP-version artifact). The fossil's bare htmlspecialchars output had no pre-wrap span, left spaces to the paragraph renderer, and escaped apostrophes as &#039; under PHP 8. Replaced with the span plus the Wikidot raw escape table; this settles raw-basic, raw-ws, raw-quotes, raw-entities, raw-wikitext, raw-brackets, raw-html, raw-heading, raw-link, raw-accents and their inline-markup neighbors. Live probes raw-basic, raw-ws, raw-entities and focused entity-apos are byte-identical to the corrected reference.

C2. Raw run arithmetic (fossil-era artifact). The fossil regex treated adjacent @ runs as one lazy match and produced a Raw token with a literal @ inside it where live removes the whole construct or leaves @ as ordinary text. Rewritten as the scanner described above; live probes raw-at-run and raw-adjacent and focused run1 through run4 now match, as do the ftml pins adjacent-after-close and adjacent-empty.

C3. Raw cross-line forms (fossil-era artifact). The fossil regex had no /s and so refused both @@x\n@@ and @@\n@@, while live accepts the first and turns the second into a space. The scanner handles both and leaves the non-empty multi-line body literal; ftml pins cross-line-close and cross-line-content-fails and focused crossline1 and crossline2 match.

C4. Form backend fatal and literal rendering (missing fossil dependency plus reference bug). Replaced the Wikidot_Form::fromYaml call with per-line Form tokens, and rewrote the render rule to emit the live literal pre-wrap form. This converts 12 battery cases from fatal errors to live-matching output and settles all eight matched-form live probes. The separator-optional regex and blank-line placeholder settle form-no-sep, form-no-data and form-empty.

No correction was made to Rawold parse or render: both already agree with live on every case the rules own. Pre-correction copies of all six changed rules are preserved in conformance-refphp/formraw/*.before.php.

## Classification of the battery disagreements

Before correction, 57 of 68 battery cases disagreed with ftml after stripping the known frame-level join whitespace. They classify as follows. Fossil-era artifact / reference bug in Raw's escape and wrapper (C1): 24 cases, every basic, whitespace, non-ASCII and inline-neighbor Raw case. Fossil-era artifact in Raw's run arithmetic (C2): 9 cases (raw-triple-open, raw-quad, raw-five-run, raw-at-end, raw-at-start, raw-inner-at, raw-adjacent, raw-closer-only, raw-two-lone). Fossil-era artifact in Raw's cross-line handling (C3): 3 cases (raw-multiline, raw-multiline-leading, and the focused crossline forms). Reference fatal / missing Form backend (C4): 12 cases, every case whose Form block matched. ftml divergence candidates: 8 Rawold cases (old-basic, old-ws-only, old-multiline, old-apostrophe, old-triple, old-wikitext, old-adjacent, old-and-raw, plus old-quote-run) where ftml has no Rawold rule at all and leaves ``...`` as literal backticks around live-parsed inline markup; 1 case (raw-quotes) where ftml escapes apostrophes as &#39; inside a raw span while live and the corrected reference leave the bare quote; 1 case (raw-html) where ftml's highest-priority raw tokenizer interacts with the @<...>@ angle-raw form and emits @-prefixed spans where live escapes the whole <b>...</b> source literally; 5 Form cases where ftml has no form rule and leaves the block as ordinary paragraph text with a typography-converted --- while live claims the block as literal source text; and 1 pipeline case (raw-in-link) where ftml's URL rule swallows the | and the following raw as part of the link where live leaves them outside. Output-shaping surface (class S): the extra newline padding around div, blockquote and code blocks and between paragraphs, the double-space collapse that turns A @@@@ B into A B, the leading-space strip on non-Form lines, and the tight block joins on ftml's side; these are owned by Tighten/purifyHtml and the frame rules and are not Form/Raw/Rawold divergences. After correction the corrected reference matches all 24 non-surface live probes byte-for-byte (24 of 24; the four surface-only disagreements are raw-empty, raw-in-div, raw-in-quote and form-unterm, each confirmed by a dedicated control probe: dblspace, indentline), and its raw fragments match six of nine in-scope corpus pins byte-for-byte modulo frame shaping (adjacent-after-close, adjacent-empty, cross-line-close, cross-line-content-fails, space-escaping, and the Raw portions of basic).

## Remaining divergences and ftml divergence candidates

Surviving reference-vs-ftml disagreements are cataloged with the live evidence attached. D1: ftml has no Rawold rule (the string rawold appears only in ftml's model of live rule order, src/parsing/rule/bibliography_order.rs); live probes old-basic, old-wikitext, old-and-raw, old-apostrophe and old-empty establish that live does have a ``...`` raw form, so this is a genuine ftml divergence candidate, not a reference problem. D2: ftml's raw-span escape emits &#39; for an apostrophe where live emits a literal single quote (probe raw-entities and focused entity-apos, with ftml's own escape_wikidot_raw at src/render/html/escape.rs as the source of the difference). D3: ftml's angle-raw form (@<...>@) and its interaction with @@ runs produce @-prefixed spans on raw-html where live escapes the source literally; ftml pins test/raw/angle-raw-literals and test/raw/basic document the extra syntax, which the reference's fossil-era Raw rule does not implement, so this is an ftml-side extension rather than a reference bug, cataloged as a divergence candidate outside the @@ form the brief scopes. D4: ftml has no [[form]] rule at all (no RULE_FORM, no Token::Form, no form block rule, and no test/form category), while live claims matched form blocks as literal source text; probes form-basic, form-no-data, form-empty, form-select, form-wiki, form-malformed and form-no-sep are the attached evidence. D5: raw-in-link shows an ftml URL-rule boundary divergence, with the live probe code-in-raw and raw-basic confirming that the reference's Raw handling is the one that agrees with live. The four remaining reference-vs-live probe differences are all class S surface and are recorded in R2-FRAME.md's Tier-2 disposition, not as Form/Raw/Rawold divergences.

## Final outcome

The reference Form, Raw and Rawold rules now match the adjudicated live behavior on every case the rules own: 24 of 24 non-surface live probes byte-for-byte, six of nine in-scope corpus pins modulo frame shaping, and all 68 battery cases at the level each rule owns (token presence and body, wrapper and escaping). The parse and render rules for Form and Raw were corrected; Rawold was verified unchanged. No ftml file was edited. No question required an assumption: every classification turned on a live probe, a corpus pin, or both. The one scoping decision recorded here is that Form's interactive submission behavior is serving-layer and therefore out of scope, based on the live anonymous page-view evidence and the loop's MailForm-style disposition.
