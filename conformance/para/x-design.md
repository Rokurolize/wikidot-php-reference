# R2-para root cross-rule interaction battery: design and assumptions

The interaction battery conformance-refphp/para/xbattery.jsonl (generated by xmake_battery.py, 46 whole-document cases) owns the shapes no single rule owner can own, and is deliberately disjoint from the three owners' per-rule batteries. Its families are: images in paragraph and text flow, including aligned images, links, captions, two images in one run and an image after a newline (x01-x08); images inside simple and advanced cells, captions carrying links or another image, and divs, collapsibles and quotes (x09-x16); paragraph formation around every block family corrected so far — div, collapsible, simple and advanced tables, list, deflist, heading, footnote block, code, quote and toc — on both above and below sides and in mixed sequences (x17-x30); newline behavior inside divs, collapsibles, cells, lists, quotes and captions, including doubled breaks (x31-x38); the two routed open items at interaction level, a score-class span and tab space-runs in paragraphs, lists and cells (x39-x44); and two malformed image openers near block boundaries (x45-x46). Every source is LF-only with no trailing newline and no line with trailing whitespace, because Wikidot strips trailing whitespace on save and saved_source_matches must hold; tabs appear only inside lines, never as trailing whitespace.

The three sides are compared by conformance-refphp/para/compare_x.py: the reference render and tokens before and after the owners' corrections (x-ref-render-pre.jsonl, x-ref-render-final.jsonl, x-ref-tokens-pre.jsonl, x-ref-tokens-final.jsonl), the shared ftml dumper pinned at f9dc0e41086de31f2be6d42d4e7e5f085bcb8220 (x-ftml-dump.jsonl), and cached live saved anonymous page views under the single prefix muse-probe-r2para-x- created and captured by run_xprobes.sh (x-probe-cases.jsonl, x-probe-map.json, captures-x.json, probe-x-create.log, probe-x-capture.log). The comparator uses the campaign-standard probe.norm masking plus local masks for collapsible, tab and footnote ids, and reports both a family-shape signature (image tag and link-wrapper shape, alignment container, p and br structure, literal unmatched constructs and visible text) and whole-string canonical agreement after normalizing <br /> versus <br> spelling, empty div attributes and inter-tag whitespace, so that the known frame-level spelling surface does not swamp interaction findings.

Assumptions recorded before the interaction result was known: the harness image configuration is the observable shape and both sides are compared as emitted; frames, footers and page chrome are outside the page-content capture and are not compared; a case whose live capture is invalid, unstable or source-mismatched is dropped rather than adjudicated; a source whose live save fails or is canonicalized is recorded as a coverage gap rather than used as evidence; where live, ftml pins and the reference disagree on a shape the three owners do not own, the disposition is stated rather than patched; and root does not edit any rule file except where an owner leaves its section unfinished and the catalog says so. The stage-start pre-correction render is captured before the owners' corrections land, and the final render after they land; both are reported with their timing in the catalog. The ftml checkout HEAD recorded at dumper build time is f9dc0e41086de31f2be6d42d4e7e5f085bcb8220, unchanged from the current checkout at stage start; all differentials stay pinned to it.
