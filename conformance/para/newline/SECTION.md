## Mechanism as shipped and left unchanged

No correction was made to either owned file, because at this rule's own layer the fossil already matches live. The parse rule keeps its regex `'/([^\n])\n(?!\n)/m'`: a non-newline character followed by a newline that is not followed by another newline yields that character plus one `newline` token, so single line breaks inside a text run become breaks while doubled and tripled breaks are left to the Paragraph rule's blank-line splitting. Leading, trailing, and all-newline sources produce no break token, and backslash continuations are removed by the earlier Concatlines rule before this rule sees them. The render rule keeps `token()` returning `"<br />\n"`; there are no options and no positional context, so the token renderer cannot know whether a break is the last element of a container. Break placement agrees with ftml's `line_break` and `line_break_skipped_for_own_line_block`: across the 60-case battery the reference and the dumper emit the same number of `<br` shapes in 59 cases, and the single mismatch, `n38-26-explicit-ul`, is the reference's gap in explicit `[[ul]]`/`[[li]]` parsing (a List-rule matter, not a Newline break decision). Around block openers and closers, in lists and in table rows, all three sides agree on whether a break appears: for example probe p06 (`text\n[[div]]...`) has no break before the div on live, the reference, and the dumper, while probe p07 (`[[code]]...\ntext`) has a break after the code block on all three, and probe p09 has `text<br />\n<table>` on live and the reference with the same break in the dumper's bare-spelling form.

## The <br> versus <br /> verdict

Live emits `<br />`, and the reference's Newline renderer is therefore correct as written. Probes p01 (`alpha\nbeta`), p02 (`a\nb\nc`), p13 (`**a\nb**`), and p14 (`a _\nb`) show `<p>alpha<br />\nbeta</p>`, `<p>a<br />\nb<br />\nc</p>`, `<p><strong>a<br />\nb</strong></p>`, and `<p>a<br />\nb</p>` respectively; in each, the reference's token renderer produces the same `<br />\n` at the break, and the only raw difference from live is the frame's trailing padding after the closing `</p>`. Probes p16 (`**a\n**`) and p17 (`[[span]]a\n[[/span]]`) show the complementary live rule: a break that is the final element of a container is `<br />` with no following newline (`<p><strong>a<br /></strong></p>` and `<p><span>a<br /></span></p>`); this is the trailing-newline shaping that ftml implements in `render_contents_dropping_final_break_newline` and `container_trailing_break_drops_newline` in `container.rs`, and in the reference it belongs to the Bold, Span, and Paragraph renderers, not to a context-free Newline token. I therefore did not change the reference spelling, and did not try to encode the container case into `token()`, which has no access to it.

The recurring `<br>` versus `<br />` difference is mostly ftml-side canonicalization, not this rule. ftml's `HtmlBuilder::br()` writes a bare `<br>` in both layouts and `render_line_break` adds `\n` only in legacy Wikidot layout, so the dumper and the `wikidot.html` pins always show `<br>` where live shows `<br />`; ftml's own comments in `container.rs` explicitly record that live writes `<br />\n` when content follows a break and `<br />` before a closing tag, which is exactly what the probes show. Earlier stages' normalization of `<br />` to `<br>` was a legitimate way to compare against those pins, but it must not be read as a claim that the reference is wrong. Quantitatively, under a frame-only normalization that keeps the spelling intact (strip trailing document whitespace and collapse runs of whitespace between tags to one newline), the reference matches live on 5 of 16 probes and the dumper on 0 of 16; the dumper's zero is entirely the `<br>` spelling, while the reference's five direct matches are the cases where no other rule contributes surface. Over the whole battery the reference and dumper agree exactly on 7 of 60 cases and on 29 of 60 under the documented normalization in `compare.py`, which factors out `<br />` to `<br>` and inter-tag whitespace; those numbers are unchanged before and after because no file changed.

## Remaining disagreements and dispositions

Class S (frame-level surface owned by later rules, not Newline divergences and not corrected): the trailing `\n\n` after paragraphs in the reference against live's single inter-block `\n` and the dumper's none, visible in p01, p02, p03, and p13; the space-run collapse in p04, where live and ftml render `alpha<br />\nbeta` from `alpha \n beta` while the reference keeps `alpha <br />\n beta`, which is a whitespace-layer question rather than a Newline token decision; the `<div >` attribute spelling in p06 and battery `n38-11`; the leading newline before code and heading blocks in p07 and p08; the missing newline between `</table>` and `<p>` in p10 and between `</ul>` and `<p>` in p11 and battery `n38-31`; the missing newline after the `<blockquote>` opener in p12 and p15; the extra newline after a trailing `<br />` before `</strong>` or `</span>` in p16 and p17; the `<p>` wrapper around a table that the reference keeps in p09 and battery `n38-21` but live and ftml drop; the `<hr />` spelling in battery `n38-19`; and the reference's literal rendering of explicit `[[ul]]` in battery `n38-26`, which is a List-rule gap. None of these can be fixed by editing the two Newline files, and none was changed here. Serving-layer scope: Wikidot normalizes tabs on save (the dropped p05) and trims trailing document whitespace, so live cannot settle battery cases `n38-07`, `n38-10`, or `n38-46`; they stay in the battery as boundaries.

NL-F1 (candidate ftml divergence from live raw HTML, already canonicalized by earlier stages; route to coordinator). ftml's renderer and pins emit a bare `<br>` at every line break where live emits `<br />`, with the trailing `\n` present only when content follows. Evidence is probes p01, p02, p13, and p14 against their dumper records, the `HtmlBuilder::br()` and `render_line_break` sources, and ftml's own `container.rs` comments describing live's `<br />`. The reference matches live, so nothing was changed.

NL-F2 (candidate ftml/reference-layer split over space-run collapse; route to coordinator and the Paragraph owner). Live and ftml collapse spaces adjacent to a line break while the reference preserves them: probe p04 settles it as `<p>alpha<br />\nbeta</p>` on live and the dumper against `<p>alpha <br />\n beta</p>` on the reference, with battery `n38-09` showing the same shape and `n38-10` the tab form that live cannot verify on save. This also bears on the frame stage's open tab question: because the collapse already happens next to a break token, its home is the whitespace or pre-layout layer, not the Paragraph wrapper and not the Newline regex; I did not patch the Newline rule to trim its captured character, because that would leave the general flow-text collapse untouched and put the fix in the wrong layer.

NL-F3, NL-F4, and NL-F5 (candidate ftml parser divergences on other-rule shapes; route to the Table, Color, and Block owners respectively). The dumper raises parse errors on probe p18 (`|| a\nb || c ||`), p19 (`[[color red]]a\nb[[/color]]`), and p20 (`a\n[[/div]]`) and on their battery counterparts `n38-33`, `n38-41`, and `n38-42`, while live renders all three and the reference produces the same HTML shape as the dumper's own error-recovered output, including the break. Live p18 gives a one-cell table plus `<p>b || c ||</p>`, p19 gives a literal color opener with `a<br />\nb`, and p20 gives `<p>a<br />\n[[/div]]</p>`; the reported ftml errors are therefore parser-shape matters for other rules, not evidence that this rule's break decision is wrong.

Questions and assumptions. The leading question was whether the reference's `<br />` should become `<br>` to match the pins; the probes settle it in favor of `<br />`, on the standing rule that saved live pages outrank pins, and the assumption that ftml pins are a normalized comparison surface is confirmed by ftml's own comments. The secondary question was whether space collapse around a break belongs here; I proceeded on the assumption that it belongs to the whitespace or pre-layout layer and routed it as NL-F2 rather than changing the rule. The tab source p05 could not be settled on live because Wikidot rewrites tabs on save, so it was dropped from the live set and left in the battery.
