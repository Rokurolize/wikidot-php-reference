# R2-inline2 rule 44 Colortext: differential and correction section (2026-11-20)

Method. I compared three sides over a 67-case whole-document battery (battery.jsonl, generated by make_battery.py) covering the sixteen fossil-named colors and names outside that list, three- and six-digit hex with and without a hash prefix, uppercase and malformed hex, CSS color functions (rgb, var, rgb(var())), arbitrary color names, rejected specs, empty and whitespace-only bodies, pairing and crossing with the sibling markers **, //, __ and --, unpaired and tripled ##, markers at word boundaries and inside words, single and double line breaks, block boundaries (quote, div, code, header, native list) and container/cell seams (span, link label, table cell). The reference side is the shared read-only harness ref-php/harness/batch.php in render and tokens modes, captured before and after as ref-render-before.jsonl, ref-render-after.jsonl, ref-tokens-before.jsonl and ref-tokens-after.jsonl with empty stderr sidecars; the ftml side is the stage's one shared dumper at conformance-refphp/inline2/rust-dump/target/release/r2inline2-rust-dump over the same battery at the recorded HEAD 751e34491193374e771142632720e17ac32e2864, in ftml-dump.jsonl with an empty stderr sidecar. Comparison totals use the prior stages' rstrip frame convention (compare.py): the reference harness appends two trailing newlines to every render, so exact byte equality is 0/67 on both sides and the meaningful count is after rstrip and whitespace normalization. Live evidence is a single saved-page probe cycle under the prefix muse-probe-r2in2-color- through the wikidot-probe CLI (create then capture, each capture double-fetched and source-verified), cached as probe-cases.jsonl, probe-map.json, probe-create.log, probe-capture.log and captures.json. A transient HTTP 503 discarded two whole-map capture attempts (logs retained as probe-capture.log and probe-capture-retry.log), so I chunked probe-map.json into eight maps of nine pages with capture_color_chunks.sh, retried each chunk up to three times, and merged only valid, stable, source-matching records into captures.json; all eight chunks succeeded on their first attempt. Fossil source under ref-php/fossil/ was treated only as a skeleton for the ##color|text## hypothesis, never as behavior evidence; the ftml corpus pins in tests/wikidot_color.rs and the frozen wikidot.html files under the read-only checkout were the primary evidence, ahead of the live probe.

Mechanism as corrected. The parse rule is unchanged: its first-fit, non-greedy, single-line ##color|body## regex and start/end token pair already reproduce live's first-fit pairing on this battery, including no-nesting (a nested ##name| opener stays literal), empty bodies staying literal, a tripled closer leaving a trailing ##, and pairs not crossing a paragraph break. The render rule now normalizes the color spec the way live and ftml's Wikidot layout do: it collapses runs of spaces and tabs, lowercases, folds a three- or six-digit hex spec to a hash-prefixed lowercase form, admits specs built from alphanumerics and the characters #(),- and space (including CSS functions such as rgb(), var() and rgb(var()) and names outside the fossil's sixteen-color list), renders a whitespace-only field as style="color:" with no space after the colon, and returns the pair to literal ##color|body## text when the spec falls outside that class. The style string itself has no trailing semicolon. Both changed-file pre-correction byte sets were preserved as ColortextXhtml.before.php and Colortext.php.before.php before editing; the parse file was copied as a precaution but required no correction and remains byte-identical to the original (its before copy is retained for completeness).

Corrections and classifications. C1 removed the trailing semicolon from the span style (style="color: red;" became style="color: red"); this is a fossil-era artifact, settled by ftml's render_color legacy branch which emits format!("{property}: {color}") with no semicolon, by the frozen wikidot.html captures which uniformly show style="color: red" with no semicolon (the only semicolon-bearing live capture in the checkout is a table-row style, not this rule), by the assertions in tests/wikidot_color.rs, by the root's static smoke and now by live probes c01 through c20. C2 replaced the fossil's sixteen-name whitelist and unconditional hash prefix with a PHP port of ftml's normalize_wikidot_color, and made an out-of-class spec stay literal by restoring ##color| at the opener and ## at the closer; this is a fossil-era artifact (the whitelist and prefix rule come from the old Text_Wiki styling tradition) settled by the corpus pin residual_color_matrix_matches_live_wikidot_dom and the in_class/out_of_class/foreground color tests, by frozen live captures showing style="color: orange", "color: #63ff54", "color: rgb(241, 194, 50)" and "color: ab", and by the live probes c03, c04, c07, c11, c13, c14 and c15 through c26, whose live styles match the corrected reference and ftml byte-for-byte. C3 handled a whitespace-only color field as style="color:" with no space after the colon; this is a fossil-era render-formatting artifact settled by the corpus pin wikidot_space_only_color_renders_empty_style and verified both by the six-case ad-hoc comparison (adhoc-cases.jsonl, adhoc-ref.jsonl, adhoc-dump.jsonl) and by the dumper's a1 shape, where all match after stripping the harness trailing newlines. No correction was classified as a reference bug, a PHP-version artifact or a harness matter; the trailing-newline and serializer differences are frame-level and are owned elsewhere.

Battery and probe numbers. Of the 67 battery cases the corrected reference and the dumper agree on 49 after rstrip and whitespace normalization, up from 6 before correction; exact byte equality is 0 on both sides solely because the reference harness appends two trailing newlines to each render. All six main driver outputs and both ad-hoc driver outputs have empty stderr sidecars. The token-mode output is byte-identical before and after correction (SHA-256 85a754572863ff7f263bb034e31ada2cb1235f429b835fcd05fe3b6f70f1a154 on both files), confirming the correction is confined to rendering. All 67 live probes are valid and stable with saved_source_matches=True, and the normalized three-way split is 54 cases where live, reference and ftml all agree, 1 case (c55) where reference and live agree but ftml does not, 8 cases (c31, c32, c33, c39, c52, c53, c64, c65) where ftml and live agree but the reference does not, 0 cases where the reference and ftml agree against live, and 4 cases (c58, c59, c60, c63) where all three differ; pairwise normalized agreement is 55 of 67 for reference versus live, 62 of 67 for ftml versus live and 54 of 67 for reference versus ftml. The live side confirms C1, C2 and C3 directly: c01 through c20 and c21 through c28 render the same no-semicolon spans or the same literal rejected forms on live, in the corrected reference and in ftml.

Remaining disagreements and dispositions. The eight ftml-plus-live cases are reference-side gaps, not ftml divergences: c31, c32 and c33 keep a whitespace-only or monospace-vanished body as a span where live and ftml drop it entirely (live settles this, but reproducing it needs the color-body space logic the reference's simple token model does not carry, so it is recorded as a coverage gap rather than padded or partially patched), c39 is the Stage 2 crossed-bold re-nesting surface the two-stage inline-scope model assigns to later repair, c52 and c53 keep edge spaces inside the span where live and ftml trim or hoist them (a Tighten surface), and c64 and c65 are other rules' surfaces (the reference does not recognise [[a href="x"]], and it spans a color pair across table cells where live and ftml clip at the cell seam), with c65 belonging to the same serving-layer scope the Strong owner inherits. The single reference-plus-live case c55 and the all-disagree cases c58, c59 and c63 are frame-level whitespace and serializer surfaces: live emits a single newline between blocks and <br />, the reference emits two trailing newlines, <br /> and <div >, and ftml emits no inter-block newlines and <br>; these fold into the already-routed M70 line-break spelling and the M69 newline-drop family and are not patched here. The one genuine ftml divergence is c60 and is raised as candidate finding M73 (Color-F1): for the source ##red|A newline * item newline B##, live and the reference leave the color pair literal across the native list, rendering <p>##red|A</p>, a <ul> item and <p>B##</p>, while ftml forms a single color span across the list as <span style="color: red">A<ul><li>item</li></ul>B</span>; the evidence is battery case c60, its ftml-dump record and the valid, stable, source-matching live probe muse-probe-r2in2-color-c60 (source sha256 cc17beb6418250943430609e8665c4ff1cbbcf3bbfd2973290e16060323a614c, capture sha256 f2ddc160eb5a6dd2e85bb10bb1847df54815ff7943a43278a8a92c54ee9c5488), with ftml untouched. Numbering continues the loop's M-style list after M72, and no other numbered finding is raised from this rule: raw-only entity spellings such as c40's &#8212; versus a literal em dash normalise equal and fold into the already-routed M72, and every remaining disagreement is a reference-side simplification or a frame-level surface. Two questions arose and are recorded with the assumptions proceeded on rather than silently resolved. First, whether porting the full color grammar exceeded the inherited semicolon-only residual; I proceeded on the evidence order in AGENTS.md, treating ftml corpus pins and frozen wikidot.html captures as authoritative live evidence, and the live probe confirmed the direction. Second, whether the list and table-cell seams were this rule's to fix; I proceeded on the brief's assignment of container/cell crossings to root's interaction work and left them as frame-level dispositions, which the live probe vindicated for the list case by showing the reference already matches live there.
