# Root cross-rule interaction design, R2 inline2 stage

The five per-rule batteries own their own matching spaces; this battery owns the marker-shaped seams between them, complementing the span-shaped subset the CONTAIN stage settled. The design hypotheses are: (1) ordered crossings of the five paired markers (Colortext ##color|text##, Strong **, Emphasis //, Underline __, Strikethrough --) pair at Stage 1 in source order in the reference and in ftml, with proper-nesting controls and the same-tag span re-nesting of Stage 2 left as frame-level surface rather than patched; (2) the full 20-pair ordered crossing matrix and its 20 proper-nesting controls expose any ordering preference among the rules, whose pipeline order is Colortext, Strong, Emphasis, Underline, Strikethrough; (3) markers crossing container, cell, list and footnote boundaries are clipped or survived by the layer that owns that boundary, which is the Strong cell-boundary residual this stage inherits; (4) markers inside link labels and headings are claimed by whichever rule reaches the label text first, and a protocol-relative URL collides with Emphasis even inside another marker or a link label; (5) unpaired and tripled marker text that Typography and Tighten see later is left literal at this stage and any later rewrite is frame-level. Root captures stage-start and final-tree reference render and token output over xbattery.jsonl, shared-dumper output at the recorded ftml HEAD 751e34491193374e771142632720e17ac32e2864, and cached anonymous saved-page captures under muse-probe-r2in2-x-. Probe sources are LF-only with no trailing newline because Wikidot strips one on save. The comparator uses the campaign-standard probe.norm masking plus local id masks and reports a family-shape signature (span and emphasis tag shape, literal unmatched markers, link and heading structure, p and br structure, visible text) alongside whole-string canonical agreement under known frame-surface normalization. A case is called all-three only when the three signatures agree; reference-plus-live against ftml is the pattern that becomes a candidate finding for the coordinator. Assumptions proceeded on: the ftml checkout HEAD is the recorded 751e34491193374e771142632720e17ac32e2864 for every dumper record this stage; the dumper has no includer, so included-content cases would be reference-versus-live only; frame-level p, br and entity surface already routed in earlier stages is normalized away in the canonical comparison but reported verbatim in the signature; serving-layer behavior is out of scope. If a question arises mid-work, it is recorded here or in x-findings.md with the assumption proceeded on, and work continues.
