What live Wikidot does. Explicit list blocks `[[ul]]...[[/ul]]` and `[[ol]]...[[/ol]]` (plus scored `[[ul_]]...[[/ul]]` and `[[ol_]]...[[/ol]]`) and item blocks `[[li]]...[[/li]]` run in the `List` rule (pipeline position 42, after `Div`, `Collapsible`, `Tabview`, and `Note`, and before `Table`, `Tableadv`, `Newline`, and `Paragraph`):
1. Tag and closer syntax: tag names are lowercase-only (`[[ul]]`, `[[ol]]`, `[[ul_]]`, `[[ol_]]`, `[[li]]`; uppercase `[[UL]]` / `[[LI]]` and scored `[[li_]]` are not recognized and stay literal, settled by `test/list/block-score` and `lb07`). Openers with a space-only argument region (`[[ul ]]`, `[[li ]]`) and closers with internal whitespace (`[[/ul ]]`, `[[/li ]]`) or underscore (`[[/ul_]]`, `[[/li_]]`) fail to match and stay literal (`p15`, `test/list/block-spaced-end`, `test/list/block-score-nested`). `[[ul]]` must close with `[[/ul]]` and `[[ol]]` with `[[/ol]]` (`test/list/block-fail`). Standalone `[[li]]...[[/li]]` outside `[[ul]]`/`[[ol]]` stays literal (`p13`).
2. Attribute allowlist and ordering: live (`p06`, `p07`) enforces the same Wikidot HTML attribute allowlist on `[[ul]]`, `[[ol]]`, and `[[li]]` as on `[[span]]` and `[[div]]` — `id` (isolated with `u-`, empty omitted), `class`, `style`, `role`, `type`, and `data-*` (with lowercase `data-` prefix and lowercased suffix), emitted in the fixed order `id`, `class`, `style`, `role`, `type`, `data-*`. Notably, live strips `title` and `hidden` on `[[ul]]`, `value` on `[[li]]` (`p06`), and `start` and `reversed` on `[[ol]]` (`p07`).
3. Body, nesting, and degraded forms: `[[li]]...[[/li]]` trims leading and trailing horizontal whitespace (`p03`), strips a single leading newline after `[[li]]`, and converts a trailing newline before `[[/li]]` into `<br />` (`lb08`). A nested `[[ul]]`/`[[ol]]` that follows an `[[li]]...[[/li]]` directly inside an outer `[[ul]]`/`[[ol]]` attaches inside that preceding `<li>` (`p03`), whereas a nested `[[ul]]`/`[[ol]]` with no preceding `[[li]]` (`p04`) or a wrapped native line-led `*` / `#` list (`p08`, `y03`, `y04`, `y09`) is wrapped in `<li style="list-style: none; display: inline">...</li>`. Because explicit list tokenization runs at rule 42 and replaces the multi-line `[[ul]]...[[/ul]]` block with single-line tokens before `Table` (rule 44) runs, `||[[ul]]\n* one\n* two\n[[/ul]] ||` (`y03`), `||[[ol]]\n* one\n* two\n[[/ol]] ||` (`y04`), and `||[[ul]]\n[[li]]one[[/li]]\n[[li]]two[[/li]]\n[[/ul]] ||` (`p10`) remain single physical `|| ... ||` lines for `Table` and parse cleanly as simple table cells without any `Table.php` edit. Empty `[[ul]][[/ul]]`, `[[ol]][[/ol]]`, and `[[ul]][[li]][[/li]][[/ul]]` dissolve (`p12`). Non-empty `[[ul]]`/`[[ol]]` blocks with no valid `[[li]]...[[/li]]` and no nested list wrap their trimmed body in `<li style="list-style: none">...</li>` (`p13`, `p15`, `test/list/block-score`). Top-level non-scored explicit lists at document end emit a trailing `<br />`, while scored `[[ul_]]`/`[[ol_]]` (`p14`), adjacent explicit lists (`p01`), and explicit lists inside containers or table cells (`p10`, `p11`, `test/list/explicit-list-trailing-break-in-block-bodies`) do not.
