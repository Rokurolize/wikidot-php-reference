Correction (2026-11-22): section 6 of SECTION.md in this directory claims the uppercase explicit list forms ([[UL]], [[LI]]) stay literal, citing the ftml pin lb07. That claim is refuted by live probes from the Antigravity SUSPECT mission (captures muse-probe-agy-suspect-f05-ul-upper-close, -f04, -f06-ul-upper-after, -p30-listblocks-upper, -b3-ul-mixed-inner, -b3-ol-upper-close, all valid and read firsthand by the coordinator): recognition is case-insensitive, a lowercase opener pairs with uppercase closers and items and closes cleanly, and an uppercase opener enters list mode but never closes, absorbing following content into the last item. The pin lb07 was never probed against live and does not describe it. The List.php explicit-block handling owes a correction on this basis, and ftml's own behavior on these shapes is an open measurement candidate (WORKLIST-FTML.md, C-AGY-FTML).
