X1 settles Toc computed over headings inside other constructs. Headings inside a div and inside a collapsible are collected into the toc in document order with the same anchor ids on all three sides, and a Toc placed inside the div before the heading still lists that heading. Headings inside an Iftags body are gated by tags: with tags=["alpha"], a -alpha body is suppressed and its heading contributes no toc item and no anchor while a +alpha body contributes both, and with no tags the -alpha body is kept and its heading does contribute; all three polarities are ALL3. A heading inside a Code span stays literal and contributes no toc item on all three sides, and a >-prefixed quote heading contributes neither heading nor toc item on all three sides. The whole-string comparison of x-toc-in-collapsible differs only in the reference's older Collapsible render surface (id-based folded/unfolded divs with onclick style toggles and plain spaces, where live and ftml use class-based divs with collapsible-block-link anchors and &nbsp; separators); this is frame surface owned by the later Collapsible rule, not a Toc correction, and the family facts inside it agree.
