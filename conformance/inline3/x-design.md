# Root cross-rule interaction design, R2 inline3 (final R2 stage)

The five per-rule batteries own their own matching spaces; this battery owns the seams between them that no single owner can own, and it is the pipeline-final composition check. This is the last R2 stage, so its hypotheses are: (1) the five Inline-2 paired markers (Colortext ##, Strong **, Emphasis //, Underline __, Strikethrough --) composed with this stage's three new paired forms (Tt {{}}, Superscript ^^, Subscript ,,) pair at Stage 1 in source order in the reference and in ftml, with proper-nesting controls and the same-tag Stage 2 span re-nesting left as frame-level surface rather than patched; (2) Tt's monospace form shields everything inside it from every other rule and from typography, which is checked explicitly including a protocol-relative URL and unmatched inner closers; (3) typography replacements (dashes, ellipses, quotes, guillemets, number/unit spacing) apply identically inside and outside paired markers on all three sides, and the M72 entity-surface residual is settled by the Typography owner rather than here; (4) super/subscript pairing against the emphasis family and in commalist ordinary prose is a Stage 1 shape this stage owns, while crossings only Stage 2 can settle are recorded as frame-level; (5) Tighten, as the last pipeline rule, shapes the newline and element-edge whitespace of every earlier rule's settled output, and the inherited Colortext span-whitespace and Underline trailing-space residuals are settled by the Tighten owner; (6) the whole inline family composes in pipeline source order, checked with an all-eight-markers source, its reverse, a three-level nesting and an all-unpaired source. Root captures stage-start and final-tree reference render and token output over xbattery.jsonl, shared-dumper output at the recorded ftml HEAD 751e34491193374e771142632720e17ac32e2864, and cached anonymous saved-page captures under muse-probe-r2in3-x-. Probe sources are LF-only with no trailing newline because Wikidot strips one on save. The comparator uses the campaign-standard probe.norm masking plus local id masks and reports a family-shape signature (span and emphasis tag shape, literal unmatched markers, link and heading structure, p and br structure, visible text) alongside whole-string canonical agreement under known frame-surface normalization. A case is called all-three only when the three signatures agree; reference-plus-live against ftml is the pattern that becomes a candidate finding for the coordinator. Assumptions proceeded on: the ftml checkout HEAD is the recorded 751e34491193374e771142632720e17ac32e2864 for every dumper record this stage; the dumper has no includer, so the one include-seam case is reference-versus-live only; frame-level p, br and entity surface already routed in earlier stages is normalized away in the canonical comparison but reported verbatim in the signature; serving-layer behavior is out of scope. If a question arises mid-work, it is recorded here or in the catalog with the assumption proceeded on, and work continues.
