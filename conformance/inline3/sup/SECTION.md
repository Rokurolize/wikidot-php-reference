Mechanism as corrected. The parse rule pairs ^^ markers in source text at Stage 1, in the reference's linear token pipeline, with the fossil's ungreedy regex; the body must start and end with a non-whitespace character, so padded openers and closers stay literal, unpaired markers stay literal, a pair may sit inside a word or a heading body, and a pair may cross a single line break while a paragraph break, horizontal rule, list, quote or container boundary stops it. The process() callback emits a start and an end Superscript token around the body, and the Xhtml renderer turns those into <sup> and </sup> with an optional css class. The corrected mechanism keeps all of that and adds one case: a bare ^^^^ is a legitimate empty superscript pair, its four markers are consumed, and no tokens are emitted, so nothing renders while the surrounding line structure is untouched. The render file needed no change.

Corrections and classifications. C1 (fossil-era artifact) changes the parse regex from /\^\^([^\s](?:.*[^\s])?)\^\^/U to /\^\^([^\s](?:.*[^\s])?|)\^\^/U and makes process() return an empty string for the empty body. The fossil's requirement for a non-whitespace body character meant the four markers of ^^^^ never paired and survived as literal text; live Wikidot consumes them. Saved-page probe muse-probe-r2in3-sup-p05 (source ^^^^\nx) renders to <p><br />\nx</p>, p06 (^^^^ x) to <p>x</p>, p30 (a^^^^b) to <p>ab<br />\nx</p>, and the follow-up p33 (a bare ^^^^ page) to an empty page, all matched by the corrected reference apart from the frame-level paragraph surface noted below; before correction the reference rendered <p>^^^^<br />\nx</p>, <p>^^^^ x</p>, <p>a^^^^b<br />\nx</p> and <p>^^^^</p>. ftml independently returns Elements::None for an empty superscript body in src/parsing/rule/impls/superscript.rs (the container.elements().is_empty() branch in legacy layout), the same behavior the Strong stage settled for **** with corpus pins test/bold/native and test/bold/empty-pair-feeds-later-rule. This is a fossil-era artifact of the original regex, live-settled by p05, p06, p30 and p33, not a reference bug and not a PHP version or harness matter; nearby shapes are deliberately unchanged, since ^^^^ with a space, padded pairs, tripled markers, unpaired markers, the same-family pin ^^A ^^B^^ C^^, in-word markers and the ^^^^ pair inside ^^a^^^^b^^ are all three-way agreements on probes p07 through p12 and p32 and in the battery. Remaining disagreements and dispositions. Superscript-F1 (number M74 for the root's M series, candidate ftml divergence): probe p05, source ^^^^\nx, renders to <p><br />\nx</p> on both live and the corrected reference, while the dumper gives <p>x</p>, so ftml drops the leading line break after an empty sup pair at the start of a line where live keeps it; the reference is the live-correct side and ftml is untouched, and I hand this to root to route, noting it is a frame-level line-break surface triggered by an empty pair and may belong in a frame-level family rather than a Superscript-specific rule finding. The super/mono crossing (battery sup63, probe p16, source ^^OUTER {{INNER^^ TAIL}}) and the sub/super crossing (battery sup30, probe p17, source ,,OUTER ^^INNER,, TAIL^^) are Stage 2 surfaces: the reference's Stage 1 produces mis-nested HTML (<sup>OUTER <tt>INNER</sup> TAIL</tt> and <sub>OUTER <sup>INNER</sub> TAIL</sup>) while live and ftml agree on the repaired DOM (<sup>OUTER <tt>INNER</tt></sup> <tt>TAIL</tt> and <sub>OUTER <sup>INNER</sup></sub> <sup>TAIL</sup>), pinned at tests/fixtures/parity-gaps/inline-cross-super-mono and inline-cross-sub-super with their wikidot.html files; these are exactly the crossings the 2026-11-04 two-stage model reserves for the Stage 2 repair stack, so they are frame-level and not patched here. The table-cell boundary crossing (battery sup40, probe p18, source || ^^A || B^^ || C ||) is a serving-layer scope surface owned by the table cell parse-scope layer: live and ftml clip the span at the cell edge while the reference lets the fossil regex cross the cell markers, the same residual the Strong stage recorded at s40 and R2-TABLE classified as not a table-form defect, so it is handed to root rather than patched in Superscript.php. The bare-empty-pair surface (battery sup18, probe p33) is a frame-level later-rule surface: the corrected reference emits <p></p> because the Paragraph rule wraps the source before Superscript consumes its whole body, while live and ftml emit an empty page, exactly the Strong stage's s18 rule-ordering residual, and it is not a Superscript defect. The two parity-gap crossings ^^A {{B --C^^ X}} Y-- (sup64) and //A ^^B __C// X^^ Y__ (sup65) are Stage 2 surfaces whose ftml parity-gap fixtures recorded8-cross-super-mono-dash and recorded8-cross-italics-super-underline carry empty wikidot.html pins, so live is not settled for those exact shapes; I did not probe them, I did not call the reference wrong, and I assign no M number, leaving them for root to probe if the surfaces matter. The anchor-form case sup59 ([[a href="..."]]^^label^^[[/a]]) is a serving-layer scope surface of the link/anchor rule: the reference leaves the anchor opener literal while ftml parses it into <a href>, it was not probed live here, and it is recorded with no correction and no M number. The div-crossing case sup56 is a frame-level block-boundary surface on which the reference and ftml differ structurally and live was not probed, likewise recorded without correction. The block-alias case sup66 ([[sup]]X[[/sup]]) renders literally on both sides in the legacy Wikidot layout; those are Wikijump-layout block forms outside inline Superscript, so no action.

Fossil hypotheses settled by live. The fossil hypothesis that ^^...^^ pairs render as <sup> is confirmed on p01 and p02; the non-whitespace end guards are confirmed by the literal padded pairs on p07 through p10 and p08 through p10, the unpaired literal form by p28, the tripled-marker leftover caret by p03 and p04, the in-word pair by p11, the same-family left-to-right commit by the ftml pin on p12, single-line-break crossing by p13, the paragraph-break and horizontal-rule and list and quote stops by battery sup32 through sup38, the code and raw shields by p23 and p24, and the comment-delimiter form by the pin on p15.
