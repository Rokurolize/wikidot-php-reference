Mechanism as corrected. The parse regex is unchanged as /{{({*?.*}*?)}}/U: first-fit pairing at the earliest closer, the last pair of a closing-brace run as the terminator (tt25 through tt27), a nested opener kept literal (tt28 and tt29), and unpaired or incomplete markers left as source text (tt38, tt39, tt41, tt42, tt43), all already agree with live and ftml and needed no change. The render rule is unchanged: start emits <tt> with the optional css class and end emits </tt>. The correction is confined to the parse rule's replacement logic and its source-context plumbing. Because the base class applies the regex with preg_replace_callback(), which gives process() only the matched text and no neighbours, the Tt class now overrides parse() to walk the same non-overlapping left-to-right matches with preg_match_all() under PREG_OFFSET_CAPTURE, copies literal text between matches verbatim, and passes the character before the opener, the character after the closer and the remaining tail to process(). process() strips leading and trailing literal spaces and tabs from the captured body as padding, re-emitting a single separator outside the <tt> container only when the adjacent source character is non-whitespace, and suppressing a trailing separator when the next construct is itself a padded monospace opener so adjacent pairs share one space. Interior runs of spaces and tabs collapse to a single space. An empty body, a padding-only body and the exact string 0 produce no container at all; a padding-only body leaves one visible space, and this is why before{{   }}after renders before after while {{0}} inside A{{0}}B leaves AB. The em46 residual is resolved here and is not an Emphasis defect: the emphasis tokens were already inside the matched body, and hoisting the padding around them is the monospace pairing rule's job. No crossing that belongs to Stage 2 was patched; the sibling-marker nesting cases tt30 through tt37 already matched on all three sides.

Corrections and classifications. C1 hoists edge padding out of the <tt> container and collapses interior space and tab runs, changing tt07 through tt16 and tt28; this is a fossil-era artifact of the old Text_Wiki teletype rule, settled by the ftml pins monospace_trims_evidenced_ascii_space_padding, monospace_collapses_space_runs_and_preserves_markup, monospace_moves_edge_padding_outside_the_container, monospace_normalizes_and_trims_edge_tabs_like_wikidot and monospace_keeps_nested_opener_literal_and_closes_at_the_first_terminator, by the corpus cases test/monospace/opener-space-forms, padded-close-still-closes and hidden-gates-pad-close-strips, and directly by live probes tt07 through tt16 and tt28, whose live HTML equals the corrected reference and ftml byte-for-byte after the two-newline frame strip. C2 suppresses empty, padding-only and exact-zero bodies to no container, changing tt17 through tt21; this is likewise a fossil-era artifact, settled by the ftml pins wikidot_suppresses_the_exact_monospace_zero_value and monospace_space_only_body_preserves_one_visible_space_without_a_container, by the corpus cases test/bold/empty-lead-space-hoists, test/color/empty-mono-body-mid-vanishes and test/span/wsmono-edge-drops-space, and by live probes tt17 through tt21.
