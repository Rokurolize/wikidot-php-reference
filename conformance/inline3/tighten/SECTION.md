Mechanism as corrected. Tighten is the last parse rule, so every earlier rule has already replaced its own body with a token placeholder and the remaining source is ordinary text plus token delimiters; that is what makes this the one place where whole-document output shaping can be applied without touching a construct's own content. The corrected parse() keeps the fossil's str_replace("\n", '', ...) that drops the framework's leading and trailing padding newlines, and adds three shaping operations. It folds every residual horizontal whitespace run, including a lone tab, to one space; because code and raw block bodies are already inside token placeholders, a double space inside [[code]] survives untouched, matching live and ftml (adhoc case adh02), while a double space in ordinary text or one exposed by the Underline empty-pair deletion is collapsed. It moves an edge space out of an inline color scope by hoisting a space just after a Colortext start token to before that token and ejecting a space just before a Colortext end token to after it, then folds again; this reproduces live's leading-space hoist and trailing-space ejection without a general repair stack, and the verification set confirms the discriminating ejected-space-followed-by-text shape that the color owner settled as c53. Finally it trims a space adjacent to a Paragraph start or end token and on either side of a Newline token; this removes a hoisted or ejected space at a paragraph boundary, a single space that a later rule's token deletion exposed at a paragraph or break edge, and an authored leading space after a line break. Token delimiters contain no spaces or tabs and each token id is looked up by type from the wiki token table, so the passes never rewrite a token or another rule's body. The render file's token() was already an empty string and remains byte-identical to stage start.

Corrections and classifications. C1 is the horizontal whitespace-run fold and is classified a fossil-era artifact with a reference-bug aspect: the fossil final pass knew only about newlines, while live's WikiTransformation::purifyHtml libtidy pass folds space and tab runs to one space in rendered text; live probes muse-probe-r2in3-tighten-tighten18, -tighten19, -tighten22 and -tighten23 render a  b, a   b, a\\t\\tb and a \\t b each as <p>a b</p>, and ftml's dumper agrees. C2 is the color-scope edge-space hoist and eject, also a fossil-era artifact and reference bug: live probes tighten40 through tighten44 settle the trimmed span, the frozen fixtures span/leading-space and color/whitespace-only-body-span-open-hoist pin it, and the color owner's live probe c53 settles the eject-followed-by-text shape that verify-cases.jsonl v01 reproduces byte-for-byte against ftml. Rulings on the two inherited residuals. The Colortext leading and trailing span whitespace is owned by this rule and is settled: C2 makes the corrected reference match live and ftml at tighten40, tighten41, tighten42, tighten43 and tighten44, and the color owner's c52 (## red | A ##) and c53 (##red|A ##blue|B## C##) shapes are covered by the same hoist-and-eject mechanism, with c53's live-settled ejected space verified in verify-cases.jsonl v01. The Underline empty-pair trailing-space surface is also owned by this rule and is settled: its whitespace-run component (x ____ y and a ____ b ____ c, battery tighten52 and tighten55) is fixed by C1 and its single-edge-space component (Empty ____ and ____ x, battery tighten51 and tighten53, plus the break-edge shape tighten56) is fixed by C3, all with valid live probes; the pure trailing-space shapes that Wikidot normalizes on save remain dumper-only by disposition and were never probed. I therefore leave no live-settled whitespace residual from either section unpatched, and I record no frame-level hand-off for these two surfaces.
