Stage-start reference render over the 108-case interaction battery (x-ref-render-stage.jsonl, empty stderr) versus the one shared ftml dumper at HEAD 751e34491193374e771142632720e17ac32e2864 (x-ftml-dump.jsonl, empty stderr) under rstrip/whitespace canonicalization: 58 agreements and 50 divergences. The 50 divergences fall into five families; none is patched here, and each family's disposition is settled against the cached live captures under muse-probe-r2in3-x- once those land and against the five owner sections.

Family A — Stage 2 crossing surface (x01-x71 and x98, 37 cases): for every ordered crossing of this stage's Tt {{}}, Superscript ^^ and Subscript ,, markers with the five Inline-2 markers (and among the three new markers), the reference emits flat source-order tokens whose XHTML is crossed/malformed (for example <tt>a<strong>ab</tt>ba</strong>), while ftml re-nests into well-formed spans with a reopened tail (<tt>a<strong>ab</strong></tt><strong>ba</strong>). This is exactly the two-stage inline-scope model of the 2026-11-04 ruling exercised in CONTAIN, LIST and INLINE2: Stage 1 pairs in source order (this stage owns that and the reference is correct there); Stage 2 repairs the crossing into well-formed HTML. It is frame-level surface, recorded and not patched. The proper-nesting controls in the same matrix (even case ids) agree on all three sides at stage start and are the controls that keep this ruling honest.

Family B — typography entity surface (x74, x78, x79, x80, x82, x83, x97, x100, 8 cases): the reference's Typography renderer emits numeric entities (&#8212;, &#8230;, &#8220;, &#8221;, &#8216;, &#8217;, &#171;, &#187;, &#160;) while ftml's preproc emits the corresponding raw Unicode characters (em dash, ellipsis, curly quotes, guillemets, plain or NBSP space). This is the M72 surface already routed in R2-INLINE2.md, here observed inside and outside every paired form including monospace shielding: the reference's entity output is unchanged by the marker that contains it, so this stage's forms do not create a new finding. The Typography owner settles M72 at the reference's own Typography files with its probes under muse-probe-r2in3-typo-; root does not renumber or reopen it. The number/unit and quote members of the family share the same root cause as the dash/ellipsis members and are reported to the coordinator as one already-routed surface rather than new findings.

Family C — Tt / super / subscript body-shape seams (x77, x87, 2 cases): x77 has the reference keeping a whitespace-padded monospace body (<tt> spaced </tt>) and an empty pair (<tt></tt>) where ftml trims to <tt>spaced</tt> and drops the empty pair; x87 has the reference and ftml disagreeing on how padded and empty ^^ and ,, bodies consume adjacent markers. These are the Tt, Superscript and Subscript owners' own matching spaces; each owner settles its shape against live under its own prefix, and root records the outcome here rather than pre-judging it.

Family D — inherited span-whitespace residuals (x95, x78-adjacent, 1 case here): x95 keeps leading/trailing spaces inside color spans where ftml trims them. This is the Colortext leading/trailing span whitespace residual handed to the Tighten owner, with the Underline empty-pair trailing-space surface (x92 family) as its sibling. The Tighten owner rules which of them the last pipeline rule owns; root records that ruling.

Family E — container and list boundary ownership (x101, x102, 2 cases): a marker crossing a div container or a native list item is clipped per boundary by ftml (and, where live agrees, by live) while the reference spans the boundary, sometimes producing a wikilink artifact from the leftover [[/div]] text. This is the same boundary-ownership surface as the INLINE2 strong cell-boundary and native-list-item residuals: it belongs to the layer that owns the container or list, not to this stage's five rules, and is recorded as frame-level rather than patched. The include-seam case (x108) is reference-versus-live only because the shared dumper has no includer.
